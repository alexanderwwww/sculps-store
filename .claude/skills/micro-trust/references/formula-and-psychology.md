## 1. The five-stage formula

Golden times for an 8 s clip: 0.00 / 1.17 / 3.06 / 4.94 / 6.11 / 8.00 [S ugc-master-prompter/SKILL.md step 3]. Mapping [S micro-trust/SKILL.md]: 0.00 hook · 1.17-3.06 impress + security · 4.94 peak + desire · 6.11-8.00 desire hold.

| # | Stage | What the viewer's brain does | Cue types that serve it | Lands at | Failure modes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HOOK** | Pattern-break test: "is something happening that I did not predict?" Motion and faces grab the eye before words. Decides stay/scroll. | Already-moving frame (mid-stride), unexpected scale, a neighbour stopping, a sound that says "wait what", product or its effect in frame by 1.5 s [S 05-directors-bible §3; hooks.md; tiktok-pacing.md]. Must read with sound off AND eyes closed [S 05 §3]. | 0.00-0.4 s (motion), word 1 by 0.3 s, product by 1.5 s | Slow fade/title card/"hey guys"; calm establishing shot; product hidden until 3 s+; speech-only hook with a dead picture; hook that says the product name. |
| 2 | **IMPRESS** | "Wow, that is big / bright / odd." Surprise plus a clear object to attach it to. Needs one unmistakable thing to look at. | Scale (person beside it), glow (cold blue lantern flaring), reveal (pan from normal yard to product), product crisp and well-exposed at its key moment [S micro-trust SKILL "slick crisp details"]. | 1.17-3.06 build; peak 4.94 with one deep low hit [S library §4] | Product mushy or mis-scaled; wow happens off-frame; peak without sound change; hero-lit studio look (tips into "ad"). |
| 3 | **MICRO-TRUST** (runs 0-8 s, never breaks) | Hundreds of small checks (§2) answer "real person, real moment" or "ad / AI". One failed cue cancels many [S micro-trust SKILL]. | 5 channels: camera, words, tone, sound, world + product contact cues [S library §1-6]. | Every second; densest 0-1 s | AI/ad tells list (glassy gimbal, perfect smile, ad language, symmetric crowd, lens-looking, slow-mo, tidy end pose) [S micro-trust SKILL]. |
| 4 | **SECURITY** | "Nothing here is sketchy; I could believe this and buy it." Low-threat appraisal after the wow. | Ordinary place and people; product identical in every frame; calm credible tone; modest claims (demonstration only, no personal-use testimonials); real policy lines (free shipping/returns) as TEXT AFTER the clip, not spoken in it [S intake-and-marketing §2.5; C for placement]. | 1.17-3.06 (place, people) and 6.11-8.00 (calm hold) | Exaggeration ("best ever"); product changes between frames; sketchy setting; "limited time" voice; unverifiable claim; a delivery date not yet true [S intake §2.5]. |
| 5 | **DESIRE** (emotional attachment, not sadness) | "I want that at my house / on my counter." Wanting is triggered by seeing others want it plus imagined ownership. | Bystanders' fascination, glow on faces, product crisp and off-centre in the final hold, ends mid-moment (curiosity left open) [S micro-trust SKILL; tiktok-pacing.md]. | 4.94 peak, 6.11-8.00 hold | Crying/drama arcs; hype voice; beauty-pose ending; product out of focus on the last frame; closing CTA spoken. |

Rule of order [C]: stages 2 and 5 are loud, stage 3 is invisible. If the viewer notices trust cues, they were too strong; if they notice none and still feel "someone filmed this", they worked.

## 2. The one-second verdict model — fast checks the brain runs

Grounding. Trust and likeability judgments from a face form after 100 ms exposure and correlate highly with untimed judgments; more time mostly raises confidence [W https://www.researchgate.net/publication/6919047_First_Impressions_Making_Up_Your_Mind_After_a_100-Ms_Exposure_to_a_Face (Willis & Todorov 2006, via search summary)]. Face/voice realism mismatch and eyes/head realism mismatch produce uncanny reactions; a mismatch between human-like appearance and mechanical motion reduces affinity [W https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00883/full and https://www.academia.edu/544236/ (search summaries)]. Note: the claim that motion deepens Mori's valley was NOT confirmed empirically [W https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010027713002114] — so do not rely on "motion makes it worse", rely on mismatch. Which checks viewers use on AI video specifically is **[C]** — no source read; treat the table as a hypothesis list for the judge.

| Check | What the brain tests [C unless cited] | Faked well by | Broken by (tell) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motion physics | Weight, momentum, foot contact, cloth in wind | Verbs of effort ("leash tightens", "step lands with a small wobble") [S library §1] | Floating walkers, sliding feet, morphing props, slow-motion, flawless gimbal glide |
| Faces | Skin texture, asymmetry, blink, teeth | Small/turned/back-of-head faces in crowds, real skin, one-sentence persona [S ugc-master-prompter block 2] | Same face repeated, perfect teeth held, lens-looking, plastic skin |
| Hands | Five fingers, plausible grip | Simple holds "by its stake/handle", no finger choreography [S block 7] | Extra/fused fingers, second finger action in one shot |
| Voice | Room acoustics, breath, fragment speech, lip match | Short lines 2-6 words, overlap, distance changes, breath/laugh [S SKILL §2] | Clean studio voice, ad cadence, perfect product name, long lines that break lip-sync [S 05 law 7] |
| Light | Uncontrolled, mixed, phone-processed | Named real sources, exposure pump, bloom, noisy shadows [S library §1] | Even soft light, cinematic grade, perfect exposure |
| Framing | Held by a person thinking about something else | Mid-action start, off-centre, late reframe, thumb edge, horizon off [S library §1] | Centred hero, symmetric composition, tidy ending pose |
| Environment | Lived-in, mildly inconvenient, stable | Bin, garage door, dew on car, leaf litter, bike on side [S library §5] | Immaculate set, street that changes between frames, symmetric crowd |
| Social cues | Other people behaving like people (varied timing and reaction) | Staggered reactions, different phone grips, someone half in frame [S library §5; style/people] | Everyone reacting at once, everyone filming the same way, anyone looking into the lens |
| Audio | Cheap-mic physics | Wind, breath, footsteps, fabric, one low hit at 4.94, loud ongoing ending [S library §4] | Silence, music bed, clean fade |
| Product | Object in a place | Ground contact, strips moving unevenly, scale cue, partial occlusion [S library §6] | Product floating, no shadow, wrong size, pasted carousel text [S ugc-master-prompter step 3] |

Imperfection logic [C + W]: the pratfall effect (Aronson, Willerman, Floyd 1966) says a competent person's small blunder raises likeability while the same blunder lowers it for an average person [W https://www.researchgate.net/publication/232576738 (search summary)]. Use: flaws on the PHONE and PEOPLE (wobble, restart, swallowed word); never on the PRODUCT, which must stay competent and crisp. Processing fluency: not read this session, no claim made here.

## 3. DESIRE engineering (no sadness, no hype, no false claims)

Mechanisms [C, composed; the repo's only desire evidence is the hook/format list in 05 §3 and the "highest-converting formats" claim there, which cites sources 12-14 of that file, not re-read here].

| Lever | How it is engineered in a clip | Honest limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Scale** | A person, car, mailbox or porch roofline beside it; the product bigger than expected at 1.17-3.06 [S 05 hook #30, #73] | Real size only (Reaper 8 ft 6 in) [S ugc-master-prompter worked example] |
| **Glow / light on faces** | Product light spilling on grass and faces at 4.94; exposure pump as it flares [S library §8] | Light must be what the product really emits |
| **Bystander fascination** (social proof by behaviour) | One neighbour stops mid-stride, another grabs a friend's arm, a third films; staggered timings [S library §3] | Reactions are staged by the prompt; no real person named or quoted as a customer |
| **Ownership imagery** | Product shown in an ordinary home's own yard/counter, not a studio; viewer maps "my house" [C] | Never "I've used it daily" in a synthetic mouth [S intake §2.5] |
| **Scarcity (honest only)** | Only seasonal true facts, e.g. a date that is real; "It's October" [S 05 hook #44, intake §2.2 "date only if true"] | No countdowns, no "sold out" unless true |
| **Ease / proof of setup** | Stake pushed in, no tools, one-hand action in view [S 05 hooks #52, #62] | No time claim until a sample proves it [S cryo/intake §2.5] |
| **"My neighbours would love this"** | A viewer thinks of the street; the clip shows a street reacting, not a seller [C] | Keep voices local, 2-5 words |
| **Crisp product at the end** | Last 1.9 s: product sharp, off-centre, ends mid-moment, no pose [S SKILL] | Crisp product inside soft world |
Without sadness [S SKILL: "not sad: DESIRE"]: the emotional trigger is delight/curiosity ("is that real?"), never loss, loneliness or pity. Hook #63 ("I just cried a little. Sorry.") is banned for customer copy (apology) [S 05 hook #63 note].

## 4. SECURITY cues — safe to believe, safe to buy

1. **Ordinary place, ordinary people** [S SKILL stage 4]: suburb street at dusk, different ages/builds, nobody influencer-looking [S intake §2.4].
2. **One consistent product and street** across all frames and both clips [S ugc-master-prompter block 9].
3. **Calm credible tone**: understated disbelief, no exclamation hype [S SKILL channel 3].
4. **Modest claims**: demonstration, features, scale only; no medical, guarantee, unverified superlative, personal-use testimonial [S intake §2.5; lessons 39, 95].
5. **Real-seeming policies as TEXT AFTER the video** (free shipping, returns) — placement is [C]; content only if true for the store [S kerberos: nothing on the store invented; no fake reviews].
6. **No spoken price/CTA inside the clip**: spoken sales language is the strongest "ad" tell [S SKILL words].
7. **Delivery wording matches truth**: no "before Halloween" until the supplier's real US time is known [S intake §2.5].
8. **Text is burned afterwards by ffmpeg, never drawn by the model** [S ugc-master-prompter block 9].
9. Customer-facing copy confident, never apologetic [S AGENTS §1].

## 6. Creative-testing loop (sourced only)

Organic rules already in the repo [S organicx/SKILL.md]: speed over perfection; every product gets 5-7 days, no traction means cut it; ~5% of tested products win, ~20 tests per winner; creative is iterations on something already proven (it ranks 3rd after mindset and research); the inspector checks "hook in the first second, clear what is being sold" and evidence the format works. Duplicate detection and strikes can kill an account, so provenance matters [S organicx].
Meta metrics [W secondary https://adlibrary.com/posts/hook-rate; https://insights.vaizle.com/hook-rate-hold-rate/]: Meta defines 3-second video plays as plays of 3 s, or 97% of length if shorter [W search summary, secondary]. Hook rate = 3-second plays / impressions; hold rate = ThruPlays / 3-second plays (secondary blog definition; vendors differ, so fix YOUR definition once). The article's benchmarks: under 15% broken (kill/rebuild), 15-25% rework the first 1.5 s, 25% baseline, 30%+ good, 35%+ scalable; its ranked hook fixes: payoff promise in frame 1, motion in first 0.5 s, high subject/background contrast, on-screen text within 0.5 s, pattern interrupt [W adlibrary]. Benchmarks are one vendor's numbers, not Meta's — **[C] treat as starting thresholds, recalibrate on our own spend**. Diagnostic pairing: low hook = rework opener; high hook + low hold = strengthen the payoff [W adlibrary].
Loop [C, built on the above]:
1. Per concept render 3 hooks (same body, different first 3 s) [S 05 law 9: 3 takes of the hook shot].
2. Publish organically first (Black Reaper's channel); judge frames with the micro-trust score before posting [S library §7].
3. Read at 48-72 h (the exact window is **[C]**, not sourced): 3-second views per impression, hold, comments asking "where", saves.
4. Kill the lowest hook; keep the top; iterate the winner's first 3 s only (iterations, not new ideas) [S organicx].
5. Only winners go to Meta paid; CTR and CPA are read there, and need pixel + CAPI first [S AGENTS §5; kerberos phase 1]. CPA/CTR thresholds: **no sourced number**; set from the store's margin when real.
6. Never post a clip that fails §7 below.

## 7. Compliance guard rails (creative)

Repo rules [S intake §2.5; lessons 39, 95; judge CLAIM code]: no fake testimonials as real people; no AI person reporting personal use (FTC 16 CFR 255/465 as read in audio report §8.5 — not re-read here); no medical/cure/guarantee claims, no unverified superlatives; no real people, organisations or film characters by name or lookalike; platform AI-label rules were flagged "NOT researched" before this file.
**What I found this session (all secondary; the official Meta and TikTok help pages returned 404 or "article no longer exists" when fetched — OPEN QUESTION: read the official Meta Business Help and TikTok Ads policy pages and replace these quotes):**
- Meta [W secondary https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/ai-ugc-disclosure-rules, https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-content-labeling-rules-advertisers-2026-reference]: advertisers must "disclose when an ad contains a photorealistic image or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created or altered" (the source adds "in deceptive ways"); disclosure controls sit in the ad-creation flow; Meta also auto-labels "AI info" when it detects industry AI signals; outside AI tools require manual labeling; minor edits (crop, colour) do not. Reported penalties (secondary, unverified): rejection or retroactive flagging.
- TikTok [W secondary https://novoads.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ai-ad-disclosure-rules, https://ugcvids.ai/blog/tiktok-ai-content-disclosure-rules-2026]: label AI-generated content with realistic images, audio, video; Ads Manager toggle "This ad contains AI-generated content" for content fully AI-generated or significantly modified; TikTok may auto-label and reduce distribution, repeat violations risk the ad account.
- FTC alignment [W secondary sepia-lab]: AI does not change deceptive-advertising rules; platform label answers "is it AI?", FTC answers "is it truthful?".

## 8. Brain card — 25 lines

20. Test 3 hooks per concept; kill the weakest; iterate the winner's first 3 s.
