# micro-trust library — copy-paste cues, scoring, and the Black Reaper pass

Tags: [S] = sourced from the real-footage study / Style Bible / director's bible in this repo; [C] = composed craft knowledge (not yet validated by a render — test and keep what survives the judge).

## 1. Camera cues (write each as an EVENT with a time)
- [S] "The video starts already moving: the filmer is mid-stride, the frame still settling." (0.00)
- [S] "The autofocus hunts and breathes for a moment as the lit figure comes into view." (when new subject enters)
- [S] "Auto-exposure pumps darker then brighter as the blue glow enters the frame."
- [S] "A thumb edge drifts into the corner of the frame for a moment."
- [S] "The framing drifts, then corrects late, a beat after the filmer notices."
- [S] "The frame lags behind the body and catches up." / "faint rolling-shutter wobble on the fast move"
- [S] "Porch-light highlights bloom slightly; shadows are noisy and crushed; colour a little over-saturated by the phone."
- [C] "The subject sits slightly off-centre; the top of the figure is almost cut off by the frame."
- [C] "One small wobble where a footstep lands."
- [C] "The horizon is a few degrees off level."
- [S] Ends mid-moment: "It stops abruptly, mid-sentence of the scene, no final pose."

## 2. Words (speech) — rules and lines
Rules: 2–5 words typical, ≤14 max; fragments; restarts ("wait— wait, what"); trailing off; a guess ("like eight feet?"); overlapping; swearing mild ("oh shit"); say what they SEE, not what the product IS; never the brand name, features, price, benefits, or "check it out".
Lines that sound real (US suburb, dusk): "Bro, look at that." · "No way." · "Is that real?" · "Wait, what— whose is that?" · "Dude. Dude." · "That is not— okay that is huge." · "Oh my god, the lights." · "How long has that been there?" · "Hold on, hold on, I'm filming." · "Honey, come look at this." · "That's insane." · low murmur: "...it's moving." [C unless marked; the style files give "oh shit", "no way", "bro look at that" [S sound-layers]]
Wrong (never): "Introducing the Black Reaper." · "Look how amazing this is!" · "It lights up and has ghost lights!" · "Get yours now."

## 3. Tone cues
- [C] Disbelief over hype: low, slower, a half-laugh. Volume drops when stunned.
- [S] "Real unscripted reactions at different speeds: a delayed gasp, a nervous laugh, a swear, a step back, a stare, someone grabbing a friend's arm."
- [C] Voice detail: breath before the line, a small crack, trailing end; the person nearest the mic is quiet, the far one louder.
- [S] Nobody looks at the camera on purpose; nobody poses.

## 4. Sound cues (never silent) [S unless marked]
Bed: wind buffeting the microphone, far traffic, a dog far away, dry leaves. Close: the filmer's breathing and footsteps loudest, fabric on the phone, phone clicks. People: overlapping voices at different distances, a close one low, a far one louder. Product: its real sound (the Reaper's lantern is silent: no cord, no fan, no blower). Peak: **one single deep low hit** at 4.94 s, reactions half a second later. Ending: loud and ongoing, no fade. [C: automatic gain pumping when a voice gets close; a car passing; a leash clink.]

## 5. World cues (pick 5+, keep them stable across all frames)
Trash bin at the kerb · garage door · a parked car with dew · a kid's bike on its side · neighbour's half-hung Halloween decoration · leaf litter · cracked sidewalk · uneven lawn with a bare patch · mismatched porch lights (warm lamp vs blue dusk) · a leashed dog that sits, sniffs, looks away · half-zipped puffer · phones held three different ways · one person only half in frame · one walking through without looking. [S crowd-realism/people; C for the objects]

## 6. Product cues (yard-scale prop on a stake, e.g. Black Reaper)
Stake just visible; grass bent at the base; strips of fabric lifting unevenly in the wind; blue light flickering slightly and spilling on grass and faces; a person's silhouette partly covers it for a moment; scale cues (porch roof line, mailbox, car); off-centre; never hero-lit; skull face, lantern and ghost-souls always visible.

## 7. Micro-trust score (judge it; 0 = absent/wrong, 1 = weak, 2 = present, 3 = convincing)
| channel | what to look/listen for | score |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | mid-action start, focus hunt/exposure pump, late reframe, finger/edge, soft+noisy, off-centre | /3 |
| Words | fragments, overlap, specific, no ad language, ≤14 words, different voices | /3 |
| Tone | understated, visible triggers, nobody performing, breath/laugh | /3 |
| Sound | never silent, mic wind, close>far, one low hit at the peak, loud ending | /3 |
| World | lived-in, asymmetric, stable, nobody into the lens | /3 |
| Product | scale cues, ground contact, imperfect fabric, not hero-lit | /3 |
Pass = total ≥ 14/18 and no channel at 0. Any AI/ad tell from SKILL.md = automatic fail of that channel. Repair the lowest channel first (rewrite the frame prompt or the clip prompt with the missing cues as concrete timed events).

## 8. The Black Reaper "neighbours film it at dusk" micro-trust pass (applied)
0.00 filmer already hurrying, frame still settling, breathing, wind on the mic, far dog · 1.17 focus hunts as the lit figure and people come into view, a parked car with dew passes the left edge · 3.06 a thumb edge drifts into the corner; man in puffer stops mid-stride, leash clinks, dog sits; "Bro, look at that." · 4.94 exposure pumps as the lantern flares, blue spills on grass and faces; woman half in frame gasps, lifts phone vertical; young man raises his phone horizontal; "No way."; one deep low hit; voices overlap a half-second later · 6.11 phone tilts up late to the skull and lantern, strips drifting · 8.00 holds off-centre, ends mid-moment, sound continues loud.
