# Supplier messages (Alex's rules)

- **One message that goes to ALL suppliers.** Short, pasteable, no names inside it.
- **Never state a quantity.** Not "300 units", not "bulk", not a range. Naming a number makes them price high and ask for a lot of money. Ask for their **MOQ** instead and let them say it first. ("Sometimes you got to be evil.")
- **Ask only four things:** MOQ, sample price, can you ship to the USA, can you do a custom box.
- **Never mention a holiday** (Chinese National Day, Spring Festival). Alex: "Fuck their holiday. Don't even mention that."
- **Tone: he pays and he needs it fast.** Confident, no thanks-in-advance, no apology, no "please kindly".
- Attach the product photos with it. The product is known (Scream / ghost face inflatable, 5 m / 16 ft, black hooded robe, red base glow); do not re-explain it at length.
- Do not offer a second message unless he asks. He pastes supplier replies back and the next message is written from the reply.

The message used on 2026-10-02:

> Hi. I need a custom 5 meter (16 ft) black hooded ghost face figure inflatable, like the photos.
> 1. MOQ? 2. Sample price? 3. Can you ship to the USA? 4. Can you do a custom box?
> I pay today and I need it fast.
