# Bad examples - look at these before you queue anything

Kept on purpose. Each one shipped to Alex, and each one cost a round of his time.

## projector-on-the-car.jpg - "the projector is the size of the car, and Jason is asking for a cigarette"

Four separate failures in one picture, all mine:

1. **Scale.** The projector is drawn as big as a car and sits ON the car boot. The real
   product is only about as long as its own remote control. Never state or imply a size that
   is not in the product record; anchor scale to something in the product photos (the remote)
   and force the projector to be a small object at least ten feet from the camera.
2. **Trademark.** The mask carries red chevrons, which reads as the film killer. Meta pulls ads
   for that. Masks are blank and plain, no markings at all.
3. **Pose.** Slouched, one limp arm held out like a man scrounging for something. A horror figure
   is upright, still and staring, lit from below - stillness is the fear. Say the pose in the
   prompt and ban the bad ones by name.
4. **The reference.** I fed the OLD green photo back in as a reference "to keep the scene". It
   dragged the old composition in (car in the foreground) and made Gemini paste the projector into
   it. **Never use an old picture as a reference. Write the scene fresh.**

The look was right (iPhone night photo, hologram made of light). That is the only thing worth keeping.

## Rules this added

- One job, one chat, references attached once, every prompt a NEW house.
- Never use `parts` with per-part old-image references for an edit job.
- Look at the picture for scale, trademark, pose and physics BEFORE placing it.
