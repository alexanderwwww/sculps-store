# Solana swap network fee, measured 2026-11-16 15:10-15:25Z (Aquarius engine)
Method: 40 coins from Jupiter `datapi.jup.ag/v1/pools/toptraded|toptrending/1h`; for each, up to 4 recent non-MEV swaps of 0.01-0.2 SOL from `/v1/txs/<mint>`; `getTransaction` (maxSupportedTransactionVersion 1) on `api.mainnet-beta.solana.com`; read `meta.fee` (base 5,000 lamports per signature + priority fee). 122 successful swaps.
Result (lamports per swap): min 5,000; 25% 8,690; median 15,001; 75% 55,777; 90% 145,000; max 835,800; mean 60,957.
In dollars at SOL $70.4: median $0.0011, mean $0.0043, 90% $0.0102.
Decision: the paper book charges 61,000 lamports (0.000061 SOL) per side, fixed in SOL (was $0.0067 = about 95,000 lamports, near the 85th percentile; the audit put that assumption at 39% of the first 53 trades' loss).
Not in the number: Jito tips (paid as transfers, not in meta.fee), token-account rent (0.00204 SOL, returned on close), failed transactions. Real cost is not lower than this. Re-measure before any live money: `tools/mob/src/aq/engine.mjs` NET_FEE_LAMPORTS.
The public RPC rate-limits at under 1 call/s: space calls 4.5 s apart.
