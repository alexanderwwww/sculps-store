## Regime (30-min windows, upside vs 6x cost)
Tier A: upside 0.38% vs needed 4.55% (2.1% of windows). B: 1.00% vs 8.49% (6.7%). C: 5.10% vs 18.68% (18.2%, rising through the day). Tier A is not tradable with these recipes at this cost.
## First five changes
1 Trade only coins that can pay: require 6x measured cost <= 3x ATR5; drop R5's fixed floor. 2 R6 off; "replay loses" block for every strategy with measured cost. 3 R5 off the real ledger until its signal shows positive median forward return vs control on 200+ signals; rebuild the whale book (buy before sell in window, hold > 60 s, no same-second round trips, >= 5 closed coins, bags counted, read more than 30 swaps). 4 Exit plumbing: anchor stop/target to the fill, refuse fills outside them, confirm stops with the sell quote, time stop ~10 min (in-sample halves the replayed loss; not proof). 5 Fix the measuring sticks: shadow probes with measured cost and the setup's own levels; drop fear from the weight learner; closed candles only; store the plan on the trade row; randomise A/B; check the $0.0067 fee against a real fill.
