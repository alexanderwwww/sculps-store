## 2. MARKETING KNOWLEDGE collected in this repo

### 2.1 Why UGC sells
- Creator-native content looks like feed content, not an ad; 90% of ad recall happens in the first 6 s; the product must appear within 3 s [bible s3 citing sources 12-14].
- Highest-converting formats: problem-solution, before/after, real-life demo, unboxing reaction [bible s3].
- Real phones win: "the algorithm genuinely prefers footage shot on a phone" and a competitor cannot copy what you shot [organicx SKILL].
- Clarity beats beauty for the buyer: marketplace-panel images — product cut out huge, spec chips, every fact on the image — keep OUR look [images]. The video does the same job: the shopper stops working out what it is.
- Nothing on the store is invented; no fake reviews; reviews/"real lawns" wall stay hidden until real [kerberos, cryo].

### 2.2 Hook formulas (first 2 s) [bible s3]
Rules: motion at 0.0-0.4 s; word 1 by 0.3 s; line <= 10 words; product or its effect in frame by 1.5 s; reads with sound off (visual) AND eyes closed (audio). Pattern interrupt = unexpected scale, unexpected reaction, direct address with a claim, visible mess/problem, "wait what" sound.
| Type | Formula | Class fit |
|---|---|---|
| Scale | #30 person walks under/next to it; #73 "16 feet. Sixteen. Look at it." | c, d |
| Neighbour | #28 neighbour stops and stares; #39 "{Neighbour} literally stopped his car."; #75 "Neighbour just walked over to ask where I got it." | c, d |
| Visual | #24 product doing its strongest thing in frame one; #29 fast pan from normal yard to product | all |
| Curiosity | #33 "Wait for it."; #67 "So this happened."; #68 "Guess what came today." | all |
| Demo | #59 "Watch this. No setup."; #62 "One button. That's it." | a, d |
| Transformation | #50 "My yard 5 minutes ago vs now."; #52 "2 minutes, no tools." | c, d |
| Urgency | #44 "It's October, you're still not ready?" (date only if true) | seasonal |
| Halloween | #71 "The whole street stopped at my house."; #77 "Halloween is won."; #80 "I'm that house now." | c, d |
Blocked by default (personal-use claims): #15-18, #63-65 and the skeptic/testimonial/gifting shot lists — rewrite as demo-voiced [recipe D7]. cryo hooks that survive: "Ice-cold water. No fridge. No ice." / "Room temperature to ice-cold, on your counter."; a time claim only after a sample proves it [cryo].

### 2.3 Structure and pacing
- **3-beat structure per 8 s clip:** setup 0-3.06, peak 4.94, settle 6.11-8 [recipe D22]. Whole ad: hook 0-3 s, demonstration ~40%, proof/reaction ~25%, CTA 10-15%, product held 1 s as end card (post) [bible s7, s9].
- Cut every 2-4 s in a 15-30 s ad; hard cuts; cut on action; trim 0.15-0.3 s each end; room tone as bed; one speaker = one engine per ad [bible s9].
- **TikTok pacing:** starts mid-action in the first half second, one continuous handheld take, strongest visual moment about one third in, then reactions; ends mid-moment, no conclusion, no fade, no title card [style/tiktok-pacing.md; style/hooks.md].
- Captions burned in post: 2-3 words/s, bold sans, safe zone 150 px top / 270 px bottom of 1080x1920 [bible s9]. Format `start-end | text | top|bottom` [style/breaking-news.md].
- Final mix -14 LUFS, H.264 1080x1920 30 fps [recipe s5]. Never auto-publish.

### 2.4 UGC creator personas
- Avatar presets in the app: Maya 24 (grey hoodie, bedroom desk lamp), Jordan 27 (black tee, living room), Ava 31 (denim shirt, bright kitchen), Leo 22 (beanie, green puffer, hallway), Sofia 35 (cream knit, front room at night), "broad" = ordinary real-looking people [compose.js AVATARS].
- Bible persona: late-20s woman, shoulder-length wavy dark-brown hair, olive skin, gold hoops, light-grey crewneck; fixed wardrobe sentence repeated verbatim [bible s4 Continuity Block].
- Rules: identity locked by reference IMAGES, never adjectives; one sentence on the face; never fashion-model/influencer look [bible s4; style/never-do.md]. Different people in a scene: different age, build, skin, hair, clothes, reaction, phone orientation [style/people.md].
- Balance by buyer: the store decides the demographic; do not bake in one [02-data-curation s4.4]. bodies: real iPhone-look girls, no retouching, no gym stock [bodies]. cryo: household-mass, not boutique [cryo].
- Voice source tag native|stock|consented:<release id>; no cloning anyone without a release [audio, recipe s6].

### 2.5 Ad-safe claims (confident, never apologetic)
- Copy and spoken lines: no "sorry", no "unfortunately", no "we hope", no hedges; a late or failed thing is fixed and sent as if nothing happened [AGENTS; bible hook note].
- No fake testimonials as real people; no AI person reporting personal use (FTC 16 CFR 255 / 465 as read in [audio] s8.5); label synthetic people where the platform requires — platform AI-label rules were NOT researched [audio; UNVERIFIED].
- No medical/cure/guarantee claims, no unverified superlatives [bible 12.2a, presets.js before-after].
- Specs only from the product record: nothing goes up "until the sample proves it" [cryo]. Delivery wording must match the truth: no "before Halloween"/"ships today" until the supplier's real US delivery time is known [kerberos]. Discounts are dollar, never percentage [cryo].
- No real organisations/people/film characters by name or lookalike [bible 12.2a].
- Do not generate imagery that purports to show contents the product does not ship; generated imagery is for SCENES with the real product as reference [organicx "learned from Magic Wand"].

### 2.6 Creative iteration loop
- **Test 3 hooks x 2 personas = 6 cells, same body**; only clip 1 changes; export 3 hooks over the same body is "the cheapest split test" [bible s9.11, recipe s5.11]. The 3x2 grid is the house formula [UNVERIFIED as a sourced number].
- Five-to-seven-day rule: no traction in 5-7 days, cut it, no emotion, next [organicx]. ~5% of tested products win, ~20 tests per winner; speed to twenty is the variable [organicx].
- Post the viral creative unchanged first, then iterate small, evidence-led (swap hook, restamp subtitles, big text hook, reorder, new music, new voice, new script); repost the winner as is (self-copy: 2k -> 5k -> 10k) [organicx/creatives.md].
- Generation spend: 3 takes of the hook shot, 1-2 of the rest; hook decides spend; no passing hook at 5 generations = stop and ask [bible law 9, recipe s4].
- Diagnostic: views but no clicks -> creative/hook; clicks but no add-to-cart -> page; add-to-cart but no sales -> checkout [organicx/diagnostics.md]. Meta is the channel; pixel + Conversions API make a campaign measurable [AGENTS s5]. Meta hooks need faster clarity and qualification than TikTok [bible s3]. Landing page is a product page, one of the fifteen sections, not a new page [AGENTS, kerberos].

### 2.7 Product page -> shot spec (6 moves)
1. Read the page; LOOK at 2-3 real photos; write size in human terms, power source (Reaper: batteries, no cord), what it does, what it ships with.
2. Class (presets.md s1) -> hands rule, capture mode, person, style files.
3. Product sentence by shape + colour only from the reference photo ("black hooded robe, skull face, metal lantern lit cold blue with small glowing ghost-souls spiralling up"); never a film/franchise name [bible s12.2].
4. The strongest visible feature becomes the peak at 4.94 s; the problem or scale becomes the hook.
5. Scale needs a human in frame [style/realism-rules #7]; finished state, not the transformation [#2].
6. Check against the forbidden list; list overrides in `notes`.

## 3. SHOT SPEC schema (every field filled; "n/a" needs a one-line reason) [bible 12.2, recipe s6]

```
product        name, shape/silhouette, colours, materials, parts, power, must-never, ref image id (one clean photo)
class          handheld | wearable | prop | inflatable      (presets.md s1)
scale          size_cm + human terms + vs-adult ratio + human in frame?
persona        count, age band, build, skin/hair, ONE wardrobe sentence verbatim, ref id, voice
setting        ONE place noun, time of day, light sources + direction, 3 stable background objects, absent[]
hook           type (# from library), line <=10 words, caption text
beats[]        t_start-t_end | action (one verb chain) | speech | sfx   (<=3 per 8 s, golden times 0/3.06/4.94)
camera         capture mode, hold, ONE move, phone look, 9:16 (API field)
sound layers   bed (wind, traffic, dog, leaves) | close (breath, fabric, steps) | product sound | peak (one low hit at 4.94) | no music
frames         landmarks at 0 / 1.17 / 3.06 / 4.94 / 6.11 / 8.00 s, same person/product/place
engine         per shot + tier (scout/final), take count
captions       exact text, start-end, top|bottom — burned later
forbidden      standing list + overrides
notes          resolutions + defaults used
```
Self-check before rendering: 14 points in [bible 12.5]. Post-check: reject on wrong product, extra person, rendered text, wrong physics, light flip [bible 12.7].
