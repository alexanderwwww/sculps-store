# BODIES HOMEPAGE

## Overall goal
The website should immediately communicate:

**This is a beautiful Pilates machine for your home, and it gives you more than Pilates.**

The site should feel like a Gen-Z beauty/wellness ecommerce brand that happens to sell a smart fitness product.

## Section 1 — Announcement bar
Bright neon lime.

Possible message:
“FREE SHIPPING WORLDWIDE — A STRONGER, HAPPIER YOU AT HOME.”

Keep it small and energetic.

## Section 2 — Navigation
Minimal:
- bodies logo.
- Shop.
- How It Works.
- Workouts.
- Our Story.

Right-side utility icons:
- Search.
- Account.
- Cart.

## Section 3 — Hero
Hero must feel like a campaign image, not a generic product listing.

Possible headline:
**PILATES AT HOME.**

Supporting line:
“A stronger, happier you — anytime, anywhere.”

CTA:
- SHOP NOW
- WATCH VIDEO

Include:
- A beautiful aesthetic Instagram Pilates woman OR a strong product-only composition.
- The BODIES machine.
- A vibrant colorway.
- Beautiful home interior.
- Saturated color/light.
- One handwritten annotation.

Hero should establish the brand immediately.

## Section 4 — Product collection
Headline:
**THREE COLORWAYS. ENDLESS POSSIBILITIES.**

Three cards:
- Icy Swan.
- Lilac Heat.
- Matcha.

Each card should show the actual machine in its correct color.

## Section 5 — Brand/product story
Split editorial section.

Image:
Aesthetic Pilates woman using the machine in a beautiful home.

Copy:
**MORE THAN A WORKOUT.**

Explain:
- Complete Pilates studio at home.
- Guided workouts.
- Full-body resistance.
- Real results.
- Portable design.

CTA:
SEE HOW IT WORKS.

Small image links:
- At Home.
- While You Travel.
- In Any Space.

## Section 6 — Workout categories
Headline:
**ONE BOARD. SO MANY WAYS TO MOVE.**

Categories:
- Pilates
- Sculpt
- Core
- Glutes
- Arms
- Legs
- Stretch
- Full Body

Use small editorial lifestyle images.

## Section 7 — Smart screen/app
Split section:
- Screen/interface image.
- Copy.

Headline:
**WORKOUTS THAT MOVE WITH YOU.**

Explain:
- Expert-led Pilates.
- Strength.
- Sculpt.
- Stretch.
- Recovery.
- New workouts.
- Multiple levels.

CTA:
EXPLORE WORKOUTS.

## Section 8 — Feature strip
Possible feature items:
- BUILT-IN SCREEN
- FOLDS. CARRIES. LIVES WITH YOU.
- FULL-BODY RESISTANCE
- STUDIO-INSPIRED WORKOUTS
- MADE FOR SMALL SPACES

Keep this section extremely clean with simple line icons.

## Section 9 — Saturated campaign banner
This is where the site can become visually loud.

Use a close-up product photograph in:
- Icy blue/cyan.
- Matcha green/chartreuse.
- Lilac/pink.

Add handwritten annotation and short CTA.

Possible headline:
**SMALL SPACE. BIG CHANGE.**

This section should feel like an Instagram campaign image.

## Section 10 — Social proof
Headline:
**LOVED BY PILATES GIRLS.**

Use aesthetic user-generated-style images:
- Mirror selfie.
- Pilates outfit detail.
- Back/side workout shot.
- Home workout image.

Include:
- Stars.
- Short testimonials.
- First names/initials.

The imagery should look like real social content, not corporate stock.

## Section 11 — Email signup
Use a saturated pastel section.

Possible:
**GET 15% OFF YOUR FIRST ORDER.**

Supporting:
“Be the first to know about new workouts, colorways and exclusive offers.”

Email field + JOIN BODIES CTA.

Use an aesthetic woman/product detail on one side.

## Section 12 — Footer
Clean white or black footer.

Include:
- bodies logo.
- Instagram.
- TikTok.
- YouTube.
- Pinterest.
- Shop.
- Workouts.
- How It Works.
- Our Story.
- FAQ.
- Contact.
- Legal links.

Potential oversized “bodies” moment can be used at the very bottom, inspired by the Experiment reference, but it should remain elegant.

## Website rules
The page should:
- Breathe.
- Have visual pacing.
- Use strong photography.
- Use bold colors strategically.
- Feel expensive without feeling inaccessible.
- Feel like a beauty/wellness brand rather than a gym.
- Make the product desirable before explaining every technical detail.

Do not:
- Turn every section into a colorful block.
- Use generic fitness stock photos.
- Make it look like a Peloton clone.
- Make it look like a commercial Pilates studio.
- Over-explain the technology above the emotional product benefit.
