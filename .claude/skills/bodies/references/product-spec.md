# BODIES PRODUCT — WORKING SPEC

## Core product
A compact, low-profile, smart Pilates/reformer-style board.

The reference direction is a small affordable Pilates board upgraded with the functionality and connected experience of an expensive smart home fitness machine.

## Physical form
Preserve these major characteristics:
- Long, low-profile board.
- Rounded premium plastic shell.
- Two prominent padded support areas.
- Two rail/cable tracks.
- Sliding/padded moving components.
- Resistance cables.
- Adjustable anchor posts.
- Handles/straps.
- Dark mechanical accents.
- Integrated screen mounted at the rear/end.
- Compact footprint.
- Foldable/closable storage configuration.

It should read immediately as:
**“a Pilates board with smart technology.”**

It should NOT read as:
**“a commercial reformer shrunk down.”**

## Screen
The screen is a major differentiator.

Preferred direction:
- Mounted at one end.
- Hinged/foldable.
- Folds down for storage/transport.
- Large enough to guide workouts.
- Integrated visually with the product.
- Consumer-electronics quality.
- Not a giant TV attached to a gym machine.

Possible screen experience:
- Home
- Pilates
- Strength
- Sculpt
- Stretch
- Recovery
- Programs
- Workout detail
- Timer
- Instructor video
- Start button
- Workout duration
- Level
- Thumbnails
- Form guidance

## Resistance system
The resistance system should be believable and versatile enough to support:
- Pilates-style movements.
- Lower body work.
- Upper body work.
- Core.
- Glutes.
- Sculpting.
- Strength.

Potential physical components:
- Adjustable resistance anchor points.
- Cables/rollers.
- Handles.
- Ankle straps.
- Foot/knee supports.
- Resistance bands/cables.

Do not invent mechanically impossible features simply to make a render look futuristic.

## Accessories
Current accessory family:
- Handles.
- Ankle straps.
- Knee pads/blocks.
- Resistance cables/bands.
- Charging cable.
- Manual/instruction material.

Accessories should match the product colorway and feel like a designed product system rather than random gym accessories.

## Portability
A major requirement:
- A woman should realistically be able to carry it.
- Compact enough for apartments.
- Suitable for under-bed or wall storage when folded.
- Fast setup.
- One-person handling.

This requirement should influence the physical design, not just the marketing.

## Materials
Plastic is acceptable and likely desirable.

The target is:
**premium consumer electronics plastic**, not cheap toy plastic.

Useful visual qualities:
- Soft-touch or satin premium plastic.
- Clean molded surfaces.
- Controlled gloss.
- Tight seams.
- High-quality dark mechanical parts.
- Minimal visual clutter.

## Colorways

### Icy Swan
- Pale icy blue body.
- Light blue pads/accessories.
- Dark gray/black mechanics.
- Clean, cool, airy feeling.

### Lilac Heat
- Soft pastel lilac body.
- Pink/lilac pads.
- Neon/vivid pink accents.
- Playful, feminine, energetic.

### Matcha
- Deep forest green body.
- Dark green pads.
- Neon chartreuse/yellow-green accents.
- Strong, fresh, distinctive.

## Render requirements
Preferred product render style:
- White or very light background for catalog/product pages.
- Accurate product geometry.
- Consistent proportions across colorways.
- Include screen and relevant accessories.
- No cartoonification.
- No unnecessary redesign between colorways.

## Reference-product accuracy
A key reference shown during development was a cream connected Pilates machine with:
- Long low-profile board.
- Two front gray pads.
- Side rails.
- Resistance cables/rollers/handles.
- Two black vertical adjustable anchor posts.
- Central circular control.
- Vents.
- Rear-mounted tablet/screen.
- Accessories arranged below.

Use this reference family as a mechanical/visual anchor when creating future concepts.
