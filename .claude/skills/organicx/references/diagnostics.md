# Diagnostics

Once a product is live, three numbers matter: **views**, **store visitors**,
**add to carts**. Where the chain breaks tells you exactly what is wrong. There
are only a few possibilities — do not guess beyond them.

## Views, but nobody clicks through

**Cause: there is no call to action**, or the video never makes it clear that
something is for sale.

Fix: add one. At the end, or from the middle onward. A line of text — *link in
bio*, the price, whatever. It can be stamped onto the clip in editing.

Worth checking: a viral creative can be a genuinely bad *commercial* — huge
views, no indication anyone is selling anything. That can still be a validation,
but you will have to add the selling yourself.

## Clicks, but no add to cart

They arrived and something did not match. It is one of:

- **Inconsistent** — what the page shows is not what the video showed
- **Ugly** — the product looks nothing like it did in the clip
- **Too expensive** for what they just saw
- **Weak offer** — nothing making it feel worth it

It is always one of those four. Find it and fix it.

## Add to cart, but no sales

Almost always mechanical:

- **Something broken in checkout.** Check initiate-checkout counts. Test it
  yourself — wrong payment processor, a field that refuses, a country not
  enabled.
- **Shipping.** If you are charging for shipping, stop. Do not put a gate between
  the cart and the payment. Build it into the price — $29.99 with free shipping
  beats $24.99 plus $5.

Optional paid extras at checkout (shipping protection, priority handling) are
fine. A mandatory surcharge is not.

## Went viral and still nothing

50,000 views and no sales means something is wrong, and it is not the product.
Work back through the three cases above.

Keep posting one or two a day while you fix it — that costs ten minutes — and
put the real effort into the next product. If there is still nothing after
15–20 days, close it.
