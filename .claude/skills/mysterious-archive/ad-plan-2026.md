# Mysterious Archive — 2-week content + ad plan (researched 2026-11-15)

The store, checkout, Stripe, offers and accounts are all LIVE (see `SKILL.md`). The one bottleneck is **traffic**, and the timing is perfect: TikTok's own BFCM data says **US users are 2.5× more likely to buy a luxury item as a gift this Black Friday/Cyber Monday**, and the top-priced TikTok Shop sale this year was a **pre-owned bag at $25,580** (houseofmarketers). Genuine pre-owned Chrome Hearts at $1,450–$4,500 is exactly that lane. This plan follows the organicx loop and the listing-images clarity rule.

## IP line (do not cross)
Reselling genuine pre-owned goods is legal (first-sale). But never use the **Chrome Hearts wordmark or cross logo as the account name, in ad headlines, or as a hashtag you own** — that reads as brand impersonation and gets accounts pulled. Say "archive", "authenticated pre-owned", "the cross jewelry everyone wants". Let the pieces speak; the mark appears only on the real product in the photo, never as our branding. (AGENTS: genuine resale is fine; deception is the one thing that stays no.)

## What the top CH resellers actually do (from research)
Common threads across the reseller/archive accounts and jewelry-seller guides fetched:
- **Scarcity + provenance is the whole pitch.** Chrome Hearts avoids online drops and advertising, so "most pieces can't be bought online" — resellers lean on *rare / archive / one of one / gone when it's gone* (houstonstevenson, nssmag). Grailed archive sellers use taglines like **"Curated. Honest. Rare."** and openly note "archive pieces may show natural signs of wear" — honesty about condition builds trust.
- **The reseller is a character.** Jack Jiang (teen reseller, ~$20M) "frequently posts about his efforts to track down pieces on TikTok" — the hunt itself is the content, not just the product (Complex). People follow a person sourcing rare things.
- **Authentication is content, not a footnote.** The brand is one of the most bootlegged in streetwear, so buyers' #1 fear is fakes. Resellers who show the authentication (hallmarks, .925 stamp, serial, weight, close macro of the finish) convert the skeptics. Macro detail shots "build trust — finish, texture of the metal, clarity of stones" (branvas). The store already has an Authenticated badge + certificates page — put that same proof in the feed.
- **Format:** short-form Reels/TikTok are the discovery lever (Explore + Reels tab reach non-followers); unboxing/detail reels 15–30 s; IG Live sell-throughs for jewelry; 3–4 reels/week, same piece, multiple angles (naturalcreations925, branvas).
- **Drive to store:** link in bio, product tag in the reel (Instagram's one-tap-to-store), "DM to claim", and the Make-an-offer flow as a soft CTA ("make an offer, I answer personally").

## Platform priority (organicx)
Instagram Reels highest (product tagging, buying intent), then TikTok (hunt/authentication content travels well), YouTube Shorts as a bonus (paid per view, nobody competes). Post the same clip cut for each. Research runs in a logged-out context; posting from the account's own tab (never research from a logged-in account — it costs the account).

## The content pillars (rotate these)
1. **The piece, marketplace-clean.** Macro on white, every fact on frame: ".925 sterling · hallmarked · 18.4 g · authenticated". Listing-images clarity, our aesthetic. One quiet line, no price in the image (customer-copy rule).
2. **Authentication proof.** 20-second macro walk-through of the hallmark/stamp/weight. Hook: "How to spot a real one" — teaches AND proves our stock is real.
3. **The hunt / provenance.** "Where this came from." The reseller-as-character angle. Rare, archive, one of one.
4. **Styled on a body / in hand.** Movement, light catching the silver. The dream, not the logistics.
5. **Social proof / the vault.** The all-pieces white ad (`design/mysterious-archive/ads/all-pieces-4x5.jpg`) as a "the whole archive" post.

## The one paid-ad angle (Meta)
**Objective:** Sales, with the Meta pixel + Conversions API that the Kerberos storefront already runs (server-side events — without them the spend is blind).
**Audience:** US, 20–40, interests: Chrome Hearts, Vetements, Rick Owens, Grailed, luxury streetwear, hip-hop; broad + advantage lookalike off any site visitors.
**Creative (marketplace-panel rule):** the authentication-proof reel (pillar 2) — it answers the single objection (is it real?) that stops a $2k purchase cold. Clarity first: macro of the hallmark, ".925 · authenticated · one of one", our logo, one line. No price, no shipping, no apology in the creative.
**Hook:** "Authenticated Chrome Hearts, the pieces that aren't sold online." → landing on the product page with the Authenticated badge + Make-an-offer, which lets a hesitant buyer start a conversation instead of bouncing.
**Why this and not a catalog blast:** at this price point trust is the whole funnel; the ad's job is to transfer the authentication proof the resellers built their accounts on, then hand off to the offer flow.

## Measurement
Live View already records views/device/source on every archive page. Watch: reel → bio/tag clicks → product-page views → offers opened. Views but no clicks = hook/creative; clicks but no offers = price/trust on the page; offers but no close = Alex's reply/price (he answers personally). Follow the organicx diagnostic ladder.
