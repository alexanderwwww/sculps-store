## What this corrects

Before this, the carousel images were photojournalism: a beautiful night photograph with
four words in the corner. They look expensive and they are **wrong for a marketplace
shopper**, because the shopper has to work out what the product is, what it does, how big
it is and what arrives in the box — and a shopper who has to work stops working and
leaves. Every image must answer its question with the answer written on it.

## The format

The image is a **panel**, not a photograph. Square 1:1 always, 1024×1024 or larger.

- **Product cut out and huge.** It is the biggest thing in the frame, sharp, lit, sitting
  in front of the scene rather than lost inside it. Not a small object in a wide shot.
- **Spec chips across the top.** Four or five small bordered boxes in a row, each with a
  simple line icon and two lines: the number on top, what it is underneath
  (3 FT LONG / RED LIT EYES / BATTERY + REMOTE / MOVES AND SCREAMS). This row is what
  makes it read as a real listing.
- **Or a feature column down one side.** Four or five rows, each a circled line icon, a
  bold short label in caps, and one plain sentence under it. Product on the other side.
- **One headline**, heavy condensed caps, top-left or top-centre, two to four words. A
  second smaller line under it is allowed when it earns its place.
- **Every element is labelled.** A size gets a measuring line with end ticks and the
  figure. An in-the-box shot gets a leader line and a name on every single item.

## Our aesthetic, not theirs

The competitors' panels are ugly — six neon colours, glow on everything, torn-paper
edges. We take their **clarity** and keep our look:

- **Black and deep charcoal ground.** Halloween night, real depth, not flat grey.
- **Warm pumpkin orange is the only accent colour.** Icons, chip borders, underlines,
  numbers. One colour, used everywhere consistently.
- **Type is flat white**, heavy condensed sans, all caps for labels. No outline, no drop
  shadow, no gradient behind it, no glow, no torn-paper or slashed-blood frames.
- **Icons are thin white line icons** in a plain circle or square. Never emoji, never
  filled cartoon icons in five colours.
- Whatever red or green light is in the picture comes from the product itself, never from
  a filter laid over it.

## Standing bans — unchanged

No price, no currency, no percentage, no discount, no SHOP NOW button, no stars, no
rating, no review or sales count, no logo, no watermark, no social-media interface, no
clouds or sky as the subject, no superhero or any trademarked costume or character, and
no word Claude has not been given.

## Product facts that must never be drawn wrong

- **The Crawling Zombie** — 3 ft / 0.9 m, low, drags itself on its arms, red lit eyes,
  turns its head and screams. **Batteries and a remote. No cable, no plug, no socket,
  ever.**
- **The Scream** — 16.4 ft / 5 m, head at the upstairs windows, white screaming mask,
  blade, lit from inside.
- **The Haunted Projector** — small white projector on a round base, throws a huge face
  onto a window from indoors or a garage door from outside.
- **The Halloween Movie Theater** — 12 ft / 3.6 m inflatable screen, charcoal frame,
  white surface, two fat tube feet, sold with the projector. Warm and happy, never
  frightening.
