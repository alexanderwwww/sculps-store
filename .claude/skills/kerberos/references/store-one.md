# Store one — the garden kneeler

The first store to move onto Kerberos. Currently live on Shopify at amboras.com and making
money, which is exactly why it doesn't move until Kerberos has run clean for a week.

## Product

A folding garden kneeler and seat: a padded kneeler that flips over into a seat, with two
handles that take the customer's weight so they can get back up.

## Audience

**Customers are 55+.** This drives the whole design:

- Body text 18px minimum, headings 36–48px.
- High contrast, generous touch targets.
- Plain language. No clever copy that needs a second read.
- The pain being solved is physical: kneeling on hard ground, and getting back up.

## Look

Dark brown `#241A12` grounds with neon green `#B6F03C` accents and cream text. A serif
display face for headings against a clean sans for body. Every store gets a different
palette and swapping it must be easy — this one is not a template for the others.

Do not hardcode this store's specifics into the platform. Kerberos is the general engine;
each store is configured on top of it.

## Traffic

Meta ads, cold US traffic, plus a 30-day retargeting audience. Ad names seen in the
prototype's sample data: `kneeler-video-3`, `kneeler-ugc-7`, `retarget-30d`.

## Honesty constraints

He never fakes reviews and never invents specification numbers. Real specs he has confirmed:
weight capacity 330 lb, powder-coated steel frame, 1 inch EVA foam pad, 17 inch seat height.
Anything he hasn't measured — folded depth, shipping weight — renders as a visible
"spec pending" placeholder on the live page. Never fill these in with a plausible guess, and
never offer example values that could be mistaken for real ones.
