# Product ideas parked for later

## Ceiling TikTok projector (Q4 idea, locked 2026-09-14)

**What it is.** A small bedside projector that connects to an iPhone and throws
short-form video on the ceiling. Three things make it its own product rather
than a generic mini projector:

1. It points UP. It sits on a nightstand or on the bed and projects on the
   ceiling, so you watch lying flat.
2. It auto-scrolls. A button or a dial advances to the next video without
   touching the phone, so nobody has to reach up mid-scroll.
3. It holds the snacks. A moulded tray or two cup-and-bowl wells in the base,
   so the popcorn and the drink live with the device.

**Who it is for.** Couples in bed, and the girl alone on a Sunday night. The
ad is two people lying in the dark laughing at the ceiling with a bowl of
popcorn between them.

**Angle.** Not "a projector". A night-in ritual: *the ceiling is the new
screen*. Gift-shaped, which is why Q4 matters.

**Name shortlist (owner picks).** CEILO · Lyra · Loop · Nocturne · UpLate ·
Halo. First pick: **CEILO** — short, sounds like ceiling, easy on a box, free
of obvious trademarks to check.

**Status.** Idea only. No store, no supplier, no renders yet. Next step when
revived: one hero render, check AliExpress/Alibaba for a base unit with
ceiling mount and a snack tray, decide whether auto-scroll is a real BLE
remote or a phone-side app.
