# income-methods — the master menu Aquarius works through

Every real way an individual makes money online in 2026, ranked, with **sourced** numbers.
No figure here is invented: each has a URL. Where no trustworthy number exists, it says so.
This is the menu the `hustle` engine picks from each run. Pair with `hustle/local-websites.md`
(the first one we're actually building) and THE MAP engines.

**How to read a row:** realistic monthly $ for a *typical* person who sticks with it (not the
guru-outlier), startup cost, time to first dollar, skill needed, and the honest split —
what **Aquarius** (no legs: research, build, draft, generate) can do vs what **Alex** must do
(identity, money, outreach, anything needing a real human/account/signature).

Caveat that applies to the whole file: most money-method numbers online come from vendors
selling the dream, so they skew high. Medians beat averages; a handful of winners drag every
average up. Figures below favour medians and first-hand survey data where it exists.

## THE FULL CATALOG (sourced)

### 1. Freelancing / services — incl. local websites  ⭐ our #1
- **Realistic $:** one local website = **$799–$1,800 freelancer / $1,800–$3,500 agency** for a
  starter site; trades projects **$3,000–$7,000** (HVAC/plumbing $3–6k, roofing $3.5–7k, PI law
  $8–15k). Monthly care plans **$60–$120/mo** solo, SEO retainers **$750/mo**.
  Sources: [leadsbylocation niches table](https://leadsbylocation.pages.dev/blog/best-niches-for-web-design/),
  [projectcostestimator plumber 2026](https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/plumber-website-cost-2026),
  [nopio HVAC cost 2026](https://www.nopio.com/blog/hvac-website-cost/).
- **Startup cost:** ~$0 (we have the stack). Domain/hosting per client is passed through.
- **Time to first $:** days to a few weeks (as soon as one owner says yes).
- **Skill:** build (Aquarius) + sales/outreach (Alex).
- **Split:** Aquarius builds the demo + site + copy + Google Business draft. **Alex: outreach,
  the call, invoice, his payment identity.** This is the cleanest money/effort on the whole list
  because the asset is built *before* anyone pays. See `hustle/local-websites.md`.

### 2. AI automation / done-for-you for SMBs
- **Realistic $:** recurring **$500–$2,000/mo** maintenance/automation retainers are already the
  market rate ([plumber cost 2026](https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/plumber-website-cost-2026)),
  review-automation **$150–$400/mo** ([leadsbylocation](https://leadsbylocation.pages.dev/blog/best-niches-for-web-design/)).
- **Startup/skill/time:** ~$0, same muscle as #1, sold as the upsell. Aquarius runs it; Alex is the face.

### 3. Faceless YouTube / TikTok  (THE MAP Engines 2–3; we run this)
- **Realistic $:** niche-dependent. Early faceless CPM **$1–$4**; a 10k–100k-sub channel makes
  **$500–$5,000/mo**; finance RPM $8–$20 vs gaming ~$3.50. Guru "$10k/mo" claims are outliers,
  real take-home closer to **$1,100–$1,575/mo** after tax for a working channel.
  TikTok Creator Rewards pay **$0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 views**.
  Sources: [faceless earnings guide](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2162249),
  [TikTok pay 2025](https://focalml.com/blog/how-much-does-tiktok-pay-in-2025).
- **Risk:** YouTube's **15 Jul 2025** "Inauthentic Content" policy sharpened enforcement on
  mass-produced AI content — quality/variation matters now. ([same source](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2162249))
- **Split:** Aquarius makes everything (whatif, leonida-daily, mob). Alex connects channels/payout.

### 4. Digital products (templates, guides, presets, Notion kits)
- **Realistic $:** Gumroad **median ~$72/mo**, top 1% $10k+/mo, **44% of products make $0**;
  a different sample says $340 median for sellers with 5+ products.
  Source: [Gumroad median analysis](https://dev.to/insightraider/72month-thats-what-the-median-gumroad-creator-earns-these-5-niches-break-the-pattern-49f0).
- **Startup:** ~$0. **Time to first $:** weeks, but only with traffic.
- **Split:** Aquarius generates the product + listing; Alex's Gumroad/Etsy + payout. Works as
  volume + a funnel off our content, not alone.

### 5. Affiliate / SEO content site
- **Realistic $:** slow. Visible results ~6 months, steady income ~a year; Authority Hacker's
  2025 survey of 2,270 marketers found **11 of 19 niches averaged >$4,000/mo** (top: education,
  travel, beauty) — but that is established sites, not month one.
  Source: [Authority Hacker 2025 survey](https://www.authorityhacker.com/affiliate-marketing-survey/).
- **Startup:** low ($ hosting + domain). **Split:** Aquarius writes/builds; Alex owns the site + affiliate accounts.

### 6. Reselling / arbitrage (eBay / Amazon FBA / retail; Chrome Hearts = Engine 1)
- **Realistic $:** Amazon FBA average **~$2,451/mo profit** (Jungle Scout), but mean $160k/yr vs
  **median $35k/yr** revenue and **bottom 30% under $500/mo** — the typical seller earns far less
  than the average. Startup **$2,000–$5,000** (inventory + tools), Pro plan $39.99/mo, first sale 2–3 months.
  Source: [amzprep seller profit](https://amzprep.com/amazon-seller-profit/).
- **Split:** **Alex-gated** — his capital, his stock, his accounts. Chrome Hearts (high ticket,
  0% Depop US fee) is the live version. Aquarius does research/listings/pricing.

### 7. Dropshipping (one-product stores; THE MAP, Kerberos)
- **Realistic $:** success rate widely estimated **10–20%** (some say 1–5%); net margins
  **10–30%** but **ad spend (rising Meta/TikTok CPMs) is the squeeze**.
  Source: [dropshipping success/margins](https://trueprofit.io/blog/dropshipping-success-rate).
- **Split:** Aquarius builds store/creative/pixel; Alex funds ads + processor. Needs money to find a winner.

### 8. Print-on-demand
- **Realistic $:** new sellers (0–6 mo) **$0–$100+/mo net**; typical $100–$5,000/mo, margins
  **30–50%**, and only **15–25% of designs ever sell**. Startup **under $500** (often <$150).
  Source: [POD income/startup](https://printify.com/blog/is-print-on-demand-profitable/) &
  [POD income guide](https://wealthvieu.com/print-on-demand-income-guide).
- **Split:** Aquarius designs + lists; Alex's store/payout. Low ceiling — treat as a digital-product subtype.

### 9. Paid newsletter / community (Substack)
- **Realistic $:** Substack takes **10% + ~3% Stripe** (writer keeps ~87%); paid-conversion
  **2–5%** (median across platforms as low as 0.62%); revenue heavily concentrated, first-year
  income "modest" unless you bring an audience.
  Source: [Substack statistics](https://bestwriting.com/substack-statistics).
- **Split:** Aquarius writes; Alex is the named author + payout. Audience-first = slow.

### 10. App / micro-SaaS
- **Realistic $:** one tracked set of 3,787 revenue-generating micro-SaaS: **average $4,298 MRR
  but median only $145**, and **just 6.1% clear $10k MRR**; first-year solo realistically $0–$1k MRR.
  Source: [State of indie SaaS revenue](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-indie-saas-revenue-2026).
- **Split:** Aquarius can build the product; Alex owns infra/billing. High ceiling, long road, real churn.

### 11. Bug bounties
- **Realistic $:** HackerOne paid researchers **~$81M (Jul 2024–Jun 2025)**, **average bounty
  $1,090**, but pay is brutally concentrated (top 100 programs = $51M of it). A living wage needs
  a top-tier, legally-identified hacker — not Aquarius-solo.
  Source: [HackerOne / bug bounty economics](https://bug-bounties.as93.net/learn/bug-bounty-economics-what-hunters-actually-earn).
- **Split:** **Alex-gated hard** (skill + legal identity + liability). Not a fit for the engine.

### 12. Stock / crypto trading
- **Not passive income — capital at risk.** We have real tooling: `trader-*` skills (US stocks,
  paper-first, never without Alex's yes) and GMGN memecoins via `ultracursor` (automated **only
  inside the caps** in `tools/ultracursor/src/profiles/gmgn.json`, AGENTS 26). No numbers invented
  here — see those skills. Do not treat trading as a money-method row; it's a separate engine.

### 13. Domain flipping
- **Honest:** no trustworthy average exists; vendor "flip for 10x" posts are survivorship. The
  great majority of hand-registered domains never resell above cost. **Verdict: skip** — reg/renewal
  fees are near-certain small losses for a near-zero chance of a hit. (No reliable source found;
  stated as a no rather than a guessed number.)

### 14. Crypto mining (ASIC) — **the honest no**
- **Economics (home scale):** an S21-class miner breaks even near **$0.065/kWh**; US average
  commercial power was **~$0.13/kWh (EIA, Apr 2025)** and residential $0.12–0.15 — so **home
  mining runs at a loss**. An ROI model: S21 at $0.10/kWh nets **-$2.70 to -$0.20/day (a loss)**.
  Power is **60–80%** of operating cost; hashprice fell from $0.12 (Apr 2024) to ~$0.049 (Apr 2025).
  Only free/solar power, industrial rates, or using the heat makes it pencil out.
  Sources: [barchart ROI models](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36419712/is-crypto-mining-profitable-in-2025-real-numbers-and-roi-models),
  [coincub post-halving economics](https://coincub.com/?p=12895).
- **Verdict: do not.** Thousands in hardware to lose money daily. Buying the coin beats mining it.

## NEWER 2026 METHODS (found this research)

- **TikTok Shop affiliate / UGC-for-commission.** Commissions seller-set **5–20%** (US avg
  **~13.02%**); common brand deal **$200 flat + 15% commission**; top 50k affiliates averaged
  **$94k/yr** (top-tier, not beginner). Platform Creator Rewards $0.40–$1.00/1k views.
  Sources: [TikTok Shop commissions](https://www.advertisepurple.com/?p=11584),
  [hubfluence rates](https://www.hubfluence.io/blog/how-much-do-ugc-creators-make).
  *Fit:* Aquarius can generate UGC-style video (xugc, kling, higgs); Alex's TikTok + shipping.
- **AI-generated UGC ads sold to brands/stores.** Pure UGC is bought outright (you own the video).
  No clean beginner median found — priced per video by negotiation. *Fit:* strong Aquarius build,
  sell to our own stores first (free internal value) then outward.
- **"AI automation agency" (AAA).** The packaging trend of 2025–26: sell chatbots/lead-capture/
  review-reply to SMBs on retainer. Real market rate = the **$500–$2,000/mo** maintenance band
  above; the novelty is positioning, not new economics. Already our #2.
- **Selling GPTs / AI wrappers & prompt packs.** A digital-product subtype (row 4 economics).
  Cheap for Aquarius to produce; needs traffic to sell.
- **Reselling AI services (white-label).** Buy wholesale AI tooling, resell done-for-you to
  non-technical buyers — same shape as AAA/websites; Alex is the billable face.
