# local-websites — AI-built one-page sites for local businesses

The first `hustle` avenue we're actually building. We build the site (and a free demo *before*
anyone pays); Alex sells it and invoices. Every number here is sourced — no guessing (AGENTS 15).

---

## THE NICHE: HVAC (heating & air), then plumbing

Picked on four criteria from the niche research ([leadsbylocation table](https://leadsbylocation.pages.dev/blog/best-niches-for-web-design/)):
high customer value, depends on local search, enough shops per metro, and poor existing sites.

- HVAC & plumbing rank in the **top 5 web-design niches** and carry **$3,000–$6,000** typical
  project value ([leadsbylocation](https://leadsbylocation.pages.dev/blog/best-niches-for-web-design/)).
- **~19% of plumbers have no website at all** (and many more have a bad one) — a clear opening
  ([leadsbylocation](https://leadsbylocation.pages.dev/blog/best-niches-for-web-design/)).
- Emergency/urgent demand (no heat, no AC, burst pipe) = high-intent searchers who **book the
  shop whose site loads fast and shows click-to-call**. The site has one job, so it's easy to
  prove value and easy to close.
- HVAC-specific cost benchmarks confirm the money is real: a freelancer HVAC site runs
  **$2,900–$9,400**, agency $7,250–$23,200 ([nopio HVAC 2026](https://www.nopio.com/blog/hvac-website-cost/)).

Why not the "best-paying" niche (personal-injury law, $8–15k)? Those are agency-saturated and
slow to close for a solo operator. HVAC/plumbing is the sweet spot of **pays well × easy to close
× we can build it in a day**.

## THE PITCH (demo-first; researched, not invented)

The highest-converting cold approach for local trades is **build the demo, then show it** — the
owner can't un-see their own new site. Lead with the business outcome (more booked calls), not
features. ([leadsbylocation](https://leadsbylocation.pages.dev/blog/best-niches-for-web-design/))

- **One-line hook (cold DM/email/text):**
  *"I built your heating & air company a faster website that turns phone-searches into booked
  calls — want the 2-minute preview before anyone else sees it?"*
- **The offer, said plainly:** "It's done and live in 48 hours. $499 to launch, then $99/month for
  hosting, edits and call tracking. Built before you pay a cent."
- **The upsell line (later):** "Want me to get you ranking in the map pack and pulling in reviews
  automatically? That's $750/month and it's where the real calls come from."

Copy stays confident, sells the dream, never mentions logistics (AGENTS §1). Customer-facing text
is shown to Alex before anything is sent.

## DELIVERY (how we build each one fast)

1. **Aquarius builds the demo** from the template in `tools/hustle/demos/hvac.html` — swap name,
   city, services, phone, colors. ~20 min. Clearly labeled SAMPLE until the client is real.
2. Screenshot it headless (google-chrome-stable) and **look at it** before it goes out (AGENTS 4).
3. On a yes: real copy + the client's real info, host it (Kerberos/Cloudflare or Higgs
   `create_website`), point their domain, SSL.
4. Set up/clean the **Google Business Profile**, wire the quote form to email/`mob`, add call
   tracking.
5. Hand Alex the live link + the invoice amount. He bills from his identity.

Template lives at `tools/hustle/demos/hvac.html`. One file, inline CSS, mobile-first, no build step.
