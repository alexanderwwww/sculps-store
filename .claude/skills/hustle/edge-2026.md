## The ranked board

### 1. AI automation / "missed-call" service for local businesses  — HIGHEST $ ÷ effort
- **What:** Build AI receptionist / after-hours lead capture / review-reply / booking for "boomer" local businesses (dentists, trades, salons). Sell outcomes (recovered revenue, time), not "AI". We already run the pieces (Kerberos, Higgs, mob, magic-wand) — productize them.
- **Who's making money (sourced):** Vendasta profiles Klint Rudolph running ~200 local businesses on an AI stack, closing in ~5 min, an AI receptionist booking a $1,400 job overnight (vendasta.com/blog/sold-ai-agents-to-200-local-businesses, 2026). Isabella Bedoya's framework credited with $1.2M revenue (vendasta.com/blog/sell-ai-agents, 2026). Chatbot retainers quoted $1K–$5K/mo, each missed call ~$850 (vantaige.io/blog/sell-ai-chatbots-local-businesses-1k-5k-retainer-2026). One solo seller: $8,500/mo from 4 automations (betonai.net, 2026). **All vendor/consultant self-reported.**
- **Realistic $:** $300–$500/mo per client recurring at entry; $1K–$5K/mo retainers once proven. First dollar in 2–6 weeks with real outreach.
- **Startup cost:** near $0 if built on our own stack; CRM tools ~$97/mo if we rent them.
- **Aquarius runs:** build the working demo bot for a named local business, the missed-call revenue calculator, the offer, the follow-up scripts, the whole asset. **Alex must:** send the outreach / take the call / sign the client / invoice. The outreach is the only bottleneck and it must be his real identity (no spam blasts — CAN-SPAM).

### 2. AI-UGC ad service for e-com brands (sell what XUGC already makes)
- **What:** Sell AI-generated UGC/TikTok ad videos to small e-com brands. We have the XUGC + ugc-master-prompter + micro-trust pipeline already built and paid for.
- **Who/sourced:** Managed human UGC runs $500–$1,200/video, full-service TikTok $5K–$15K/mo (hirevire.com/blog/cost-to-hire-tiktok-creators, 2026). AI generators undercut at $0.30–$5/video (crepal.ai, videotok.app, 2026) — that gap IS the margin. Fiverr sellers list AI UGC ad gigs live (fiverr.com/traycyray/create-ai-ugc-video-ads-for-brands). **Vendor pricing, favors their tools; TikTok AI-content policy must be checked per account.**
- **Realistic $:** $50–$300/video or $500–$2K/mo retainer; our cost is pennies of credits. First dollar 1–3 weeks.
- **Startup cost:** credits only (keep to 480p/short shots — AGENTS 27, never generate before Alex says "generate").
- **Aquarius runs:** the whole ad (prompt, generate on Alex's "generate", filter, deliver), a portfolio reel, the gig copy. **Alex must:** the account/profile (Fiverr/Upwork/outreach), payout, posting the gig.

### 3. Faceless TikTok Shop affiliate (no inventory, no checkout risk)
- **What:** Faceless product-demo / voiceover / hands-only videos tagged to TikTok Shop products; earn commission on sales. No stock, no processor, no checkout exposure (sidesteps our payments scars entirely).
- **Who/sourced:** Commission set by seller 1%–80%, US avg 13.02%; typical 5–20% (outfy.com/blog/tiktok-affiliate-marketing, nealschaffer.com, 2026). Full affiliate access at 5,000 followers (virlo.ai/blog/tiktok-shop-affiliate-program, 2026). Faceless formats (screen-record, hands-only, voiceover unboxing) explicitly qualify (clippie.ai/blog/tiktok-shop-faceless-creator-monetisation-2026). Claimed 800K+ US affiliate creators (unverified secondary). **Earnings = volume × commission; no verified faceless averages.**
- **Realistic $:** slow — illustrative $500 sales × 10% = $50; real money needs a video that hits. First dollar weeks–months.
- **Startup cost:** $0.
- **Aquarius runs:** script, voiceover, edit the faceless clips, pick products by demand (depop-scout method transfers). **Alex must:** the TikTok account in his name + posting (we don't post as him without OK), reach 5K followers, link bank.

### 4. AI micro-SaaS / vibe-coded tool (one narrow painful task)
- **What:** Ship a tiny single-job tool (we can vibe-code on Cloudflare Workers like Kerberos/mob). Pick ONE customer, ONE repeated task.
- **Who/sourced:** Marc Lou built TrustMRR in a day with AI tools, out-earned his prior 3 projects (talesontech.pages.dev, 2026, secondary). Reality check: ~70% of micro-SaaS earn under $1,000 MRR, top 1% cross $50K (dodopayments.com/blogs/micro-saas-ideas-2026). Example: 89 customers × $75 = $6,675 MRR Q1 2026 (self-reported). **Risk: 45% of AI-generated code has security vulns (secondhand) — checkout/tenant-isolation bugs are real; our AGENTS 20 checkout law applies if it ever charges.**
- **Realistic $:** most land <$1K MRR; a good one $2K–$6K MRR in months.
- **Startup cost:** ~$0 on our Cloudflare stack.
- **Aquarius runs:** build, deploy, land page, copy. **Alex must:** Stripe/processor (payments skill checklist first), domain, distribution.

- **What:** Paid AI model evaluation / data labeling. Reliable but trades Alex's time for hours; Aquarius can't do the human-identity work.
- **Who/sourced:** Mercor claims >$1.5M/day to 30K+ experts, avg $85+/hr (TechCrunch via herohunt.ai/blog/mercor-2026). - **Aquarius runs:** nothing autonomous (needs Alex's verified identity + his hands). Good fallback cash, bad fit for a no-legs agent.

### 7. Newsletter + AI (niche, with product/membership attached)
- **What:** AI-assisted niche newsletter, monetized by membership/digital product (ads pay too little alone).
- **Who/sourced:** Sources agree memberships/communities are more stable than ad revenue; Facebook/community creators self-report $10K–$35K/mo (nbot.ai trackers, unnamed sources). AI writing alone $0–$500/mo for first 6 months (sidehustleschool.com). **Mostly unverified; long ramp.**
- **Realistic $:** slow build, 6+ months to meaningful money.
- **Aquarius runs:** write every issue, build the product. **Alex must:** the sending identity, payout, the audience.
