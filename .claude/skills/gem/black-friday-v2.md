## How to read this
- **Cost** = AliExpress US search card, sorted by orders, fetched 2026-11-15 with its URL. AliExpress shows different prices on repeat fetches (new-user / A-B bait); the realistic card price is used, $0.33–$1.33 bait cards ignored.
- **Price** = suggested, anchored to the TikTok median ($40, two-thirds of top products under $50 — the BF impulse zone is **$25–$65**, houseofmarketers/dark-room).
- **Gross %** = (price − cost) ÷ price, before ads/fees/returns.
- **Demand** 3 = named in TikTok BFCM data with a cited % or $; 2 = retail best-seller or 300+ sold; 1 = thin.
- Ship note: "6 d" = the card showed "Fastest delivery in 6 days" to the US. For a customer to get it by BF the order must be placed ~now; for most BF stores the sale happens BF weekend and ships after, which is fine for impulse buyers who expect a few days.

## The signal, in one line
The single clearest BF data point is **pre-owned luxury: US users are 2.5× more likely to buy a luxury item as a gift this BFCM, and TikTok Shop's top-priced sale this year was a pre-owned bag at $25,580** (houseofmarketers). That is Mysterious Archive's lane, with zero product cost — his own genuine Chrome Hearts stock. Jewelry is the second clearest: **gold jewelry searches +1,134% MoM, opal rings +825%, #giftsforwomen +440%**.
