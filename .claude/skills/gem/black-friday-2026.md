## How to read this
- **Cost** = AliExpress US search card price on 2026-11-15, sorted by orders. AliExpress showed DIFFERENT prices for the same item on repeat fetches (e.g. U'King snow machine $170.00 then $38.68; neck massager $20.79 then $50.47) — new-user/A-B pricing. Where two prices were seen, the HIGHER one is used. $0.33 cards are new-user bait and ignored. Confirm in a logged-in session before buying.
- **Price** is my suggestion, not a fetched number. Anchors are listed where I found them.
- **Gross margin** = (price − cost) ÷ price. Excludes ads, processor fees (~3%), returns.
- **Score** = gross $ × demand (1–3) ÷ effort (1–5). Demand 3 = TikTok/data cites the item and 10K+ sold; 2 = retail best-seller tags or 300+ sold; 1 = thin.
