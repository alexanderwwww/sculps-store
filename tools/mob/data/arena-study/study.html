5 · GROK · HIS MACHINE

HIS WALLET, THE LAST 240 REPORTS HE SENT, SOL

Our server kept only his last 240 reports (about 192 minutes). His full ledger, one row per trade, is frozen on his machine and has not been sent to ours, so his chart for the whole run and his own spectrogram cannot be drawn yet.

WHAT HIS MACHINE FOUND (his own reports in the war room; edge = points of % per trade over his coin flip)

  • Fibonacci on new pairs: 50% level +1.17 (492 trades, range +0.57 to +1.77), 38.2% level +1.04 (526). Reported 18:03.
  • Following whale buys: +0.39 (700 trades, range +0.18 to +0.60). Reported 18:03.
  • A coin's address first seen on X under 15 minutes ago: +0.69 at 868 trades, +0.54 at 1,059, then faded to -0.09 at 1,657. It did not hold.
  • Raw X heat: decayed to +0.18 with the range crossing zero.
  • Breakouts and momentum: his worst too (-0.89 and -0.75 against his coin flip). Both machines agree.
  • Dips on brand-new or thin pairs lose (-2.15 against his coin flip on 176 early trades).
  • His costs run harsher than ours: his coin flip lost about 1.8 to 1.9% a trade, ours 0.5%.
  • His machine pauses between his sweeps, so it only traded while he was awake; that is why he closed 202,395 trades to our 664,164.

6 · WHAT THE RUN SAYS

HELD ALL DAY, ON BOTH MACHINES

  • Chasing strength loses. Breakouts and momentum were the worst rules on Aquarius (62,648 live trades) and on Grok.
  • The cost is the wall: about 0.47% a round trip on ours. Before costs our trades were roughly flat.

HELD ALL DAY, ON AQUARIUS

  • Buying a dip beats the coin flip by about 0.35 points on 28,519 live trades, in every read since the first night.
  • The dip works better the longer it is held (2 to 45 minutes) and on pools from $100K to $500K.
  • Most coins on the list moved too little to cover the cost.

LOOKED GOOD, THEN DID NOT HOLD

  • Fibonacci on Aquarius: in profit on about 1,000 trades at 02:24, a loss by 04:20.
  • Dip in an up-trend: +0.20% on 404 trades at 11:50, -0.41% two hours later.
  • Grok's early-X rule: +0.69 at 868 trades, -0.09 at 1,657.

STILL OPEN

  • Nothing made money after costs on either machine.
  • Dips on coins that move 2% or more: +0.30% and +2.25% a trade, on only 184 trades.
  • Grok's Fibonacci on new pairs and whale-following: positive on 500 to 700 trades each, not yet repeated.
  • The two machines disagree on Fibonacci and on dips for new pairs. Different coins and different costs; one study on both ledgers settles it.
