# never-do.md
The Avoid list. It grows from your 👎 videos: say what looked fake and the line is added here.

## Never
- Anyone who looks like a fashion model, an influencer, a stock photo or an actor
- Every person looking the same: same face, same clothes, same age, same hair, same expression
- Perfect teeth, perfect hair, perfect skin, perfect symmetry, airbrushed faces
- Cinematic colour grading, teal-and-orange, film grain overlays, lens flares, shallow-depth-of-field bokeh
- Smooth gimbal, drone, dolly, crane or tracking shots, perfectly level horizon, perfectly centred subject
- Studio lighting, softboxes, rim lights, volumetric god rays, glossy product-ad lighting
- Text, captions, logos, watermarks, subtitles, UI overlays or numbers drawn into the picture
- A person in a costume or robe standing in for an inflatable: inflatables have no legs and no feet, a puffy wrinkled shape
- Cartoon, CGI, video-game or 3D-render look, plastic skin, waxy faces, uncanny eyes
- Extra fingers, fused fingers, melting hands, hands that change shape, warped faces, faces that morph between shots
- Crowds that are blurred blobs, copy-pasted people, people with no phones when others film
- Silence, studio-clean audio, sudden volume jumps without a cause, robotic voices, voiceover narration, laugh tracks
- A product that changes size, colour, shape or design from one moment to the next
- Slow-motion, time-lapse, speed ramps, whip-pan transitions, jump-cut effects, zoom-in effects, motion blur on everything
- Dialogue that sounds written or scripted, perfect line delivery, advertising language
