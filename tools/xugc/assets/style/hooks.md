# hooks.md
The first two seconds decide everything.

## Prompt
- Opens already mid-action, as if she just started filming, no intro, no logo
- She looks at the camera and says something personal and specific in her first sentence
- The product appears within the first three seconds
- Ends abruptly, the way a real person stops recording

## Never
- Slow fades, title cards, "hey guys welcome back", a perfect closing pose
