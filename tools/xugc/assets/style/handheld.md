# handheld.md
Camera movement. Real UGC is held by a hand.

## Prompt
- Phone held by hand at arm's length or chest height, never on a tripod
- Constant small hand shake, the framing drifts slowly every two or three seconds
- When she moves the frame lags a beat and then catches up
- She glances at the screen, then back to the lens
- Reframes quickly when the product is shown

## Never
- Smooth push-ins, pans, gimbal glides, perfectly level horizon, perfectly centred subject
