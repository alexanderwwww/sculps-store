# phone-flaws.md
The small imperfections that make footage believable.

## Prompt
- The autofocus hunts and breathes for a moment when something new comes into view
- Auto-exposure pumps brighter and darker when the red glow or porch lights enter the frame
- A faint rolling-shutter wobble when the phone moves fast, and motion smear on quick pans
- Highlights from porch lights bloom slightly, shadows are noisy and crushed, colours slightly oversaturated by the phone
- A thumb or finger edge intrudes at the frame edge for a moment, the framing drifts and corrects late
- Digital zoom crops in a little when the filmer tries to get closer, the image gets softer and more compressed

## Never
- A perfectly sharp, perfectly exposed, perfectly stable image
