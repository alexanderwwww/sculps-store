# iphone-look.md
How the picture must look so it reads as a real phone video and not an advert.

## Prompt
- Shot on an iPhone, vertical 9:16, ordinary phone-camera colour and contrast
- Natural available light only, a lamp or window, never studio lighting
- Slight sensor noise in the shadows, mild auto-exposure shifts when she moves
- Skin looks real: pores, small flushes, no airbrushing
- Slightly wide phone lens, the background is a real lived-in room

## Never
- Cinematic colour grading, shallow-depth-of-field bokeh, lens flares, studio backdrop
