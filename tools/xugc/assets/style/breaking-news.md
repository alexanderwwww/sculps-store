# breaking-news.md
The hook. It is a CAPTION on the screen for the first seconds, never a spoken line and never an anchor on a TV.
The video model cannot draw reliable text, so these lines are burned onto the finished video by the app.
Format: start-end in seconds | text | top or bottom (top is the default).

## Captions
- 0-4 | BREAKING NEWS
- 0.6-4 | A neighbour just did something insane | top

## Prompt
- It feels like the first few seconds of an urgent neighbourhood-news clip someone filmed on their phone, with a rushed, excited energy
- The filmer is out of breath and moving fast toward something, as if they just heard something was happening

## Never
- A TV studio, a news anchor, a news desk, a ticker or a broadcast graphic drawn into the picture
- A spoken "breaking news" line
