# people.md
Every person must look and act different. Real streets are not casting calls.

## Prompt
- Every person is clearly different from the others: different age, build, skin tone, hair, clothes and posture
- Mix of ages from small children to people in their sixties, ordinary bodies, ordinary clothes, nothing coordinated
- Each person reacts in their own way: one laughs, one swears under their breath, one steps back, one stares silently, one films
- Real people hold their phones differently: one vertical, one horizontal, one with two hands, one at arm's length
- Some people are half out of frame, partly hidden behind others, looking the wrong way, or walking through the shot
