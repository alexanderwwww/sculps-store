# tiktok-pacing.md
How a real viral clip moves.

## Prompt
- It starts mid-action in the first half second with something already happening, no establishing shot
- One continuous handheld take, the camera whips toward whatever the filmer notices next
- The strongest visual moment lands at about one third of the way in, then the reactions keep it going
- It ends without a conclusion, mid-moment, as if the filmer simply stopped recording

## Never
- An intro, an outro, a fade, a title card, a calm opening, a tidy ending
