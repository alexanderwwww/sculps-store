# inflatable-physics.md
A giant inflatable decoration is fabric and air, not an actor.

## Prompt
- The figure is an inflatable: smooth puffy nylon fabric with soft wrinkles, seam lines, taut tie-down ropes pulled to stakes in the grass, and a hose running to a roaring electric blower
- It has no legs and no feet: the long robe is one inflated cone that widens to the ground
- While inflating it lies flat and wrinkled, then rises in jerky stages, the head tipping up last, and the fabric shivers and ripples
- When standing it sways slowly in the wind, the sleeves and ragged strips flutter, and the whole shape leans a little on its ropes
- A warm red light inside makes the robe glow, bright near the bottom and fading upward, and spills onto the grass
- Its scale is huge: an adult is only as tall as its knees-to-hips, and its head is level with the upstairs windows

## Never
- A real person in a costume, a person inside the robe, visible legs, feet or shoes, a human body shape
- A rigid statue, a solid prop, a mannequin, a stiff plastic figure
