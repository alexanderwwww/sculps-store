# crowd-realism.md
Real crowds on a real street. No clones.

## Prompt
- Between six and ten different named-by-look people: a teenager in a hoodie, a woman carrying a toddler, an older man in a flat cap, two kids in costumes, a man in a high-vis jacket, a woman in scrubs, a dad with a pushchair
- Different heights, weights, skin tones, hair, glasses, beards, clothing colours and posture for every single person
- At least three people hold their phones up and film it, each at a different angle and a different phone orientation
- One person is only half in the frame, one walks through the shot without looking, one stands behind another and peers over their shoulder
- Real unscripted reactions at different speeds: a delayed gasp, a nervous laugh, a swear, a step back, a stare, someone grabbing a friend's arm
- People arrive and leave during the shot, nobody stands in a neat line, nobody looks at the camera on purpose

## Never
- The same person repeated, twins, identical faces, identical outfits, identical poses, mirrored crowds
- A neat semicircle of people, everyone facing the same way, everyone reacting at the same moment
