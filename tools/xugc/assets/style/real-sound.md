# real-sound.md
Audio. It is never silent and never quiet, from the very first frame to the last.

## Prompt
- Sound is present and clearly audible from the first frame to the last frame, with no quiet gaps and no fade out at the end
- A constant bed of phone-microphone ambience: wind brushing the microphone, distant traffic, a dog, leaves, people murmuring
- Close sounds are loud and close: breathing, fabric rubbing on the phone, footsteps on wet leaves, a phone shutter click
- The product makes its own real sound: a blower roar, fabric flapping, a motor hum, a zip, a thump
- People speak over each other in short imperfect sentences, with swearing, laughter, gasps and "oh shit", "no way", "bro look at that"
- Voices come from different distances: one close to the microphone, others further away
- One single deep low-frequency hit at the biggest moment of the video

## Never
- Silence, studio-clean voice, narration, voiceover, canned effects, a laugh track
