# real-sound.md
Audio. Dead silence is the giveaway that a video is fake.

## Prompt
- Phone-microphone audio: faint room tone and distant street sound the whole time
- She speaks naturally in short, slightly imperfect sentences with small breaths and pauses
- Product sounds are real: a click, a plug going in, a fan hum
- No music bed, no voiceover, no sound effects that a person could not hear in the room

## Never
- Studio-clean voice, silence between words, background music
