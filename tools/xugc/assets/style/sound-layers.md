# sound-layers.md
What the microphone hears, layer by layer, second by second.

## Prompt
- From the first frame: wind buffeting the phone microphone, distant traffic, a dog barking far away, dry leaves rustling underfoot
- The filmer's own breathing, footsteps and fabric brushing the phone are the loudest close sounds
- During the inflation: a loud electric blower roar that rises in pitch, fabric flapping and snapping, ropes creaking
- At the stand-up moment: one deep bass hit, and every person reacts a half-second later with gasps, laughs and swearing
- During the reaction: overlapping voices, a child shouting excitedly, an adult saying "oh shit" and "bro look at that", phones clicking
- The ending is loud and ongoing, never a fade out: crowd noise, wind, the blower still running

## Never
- Silence at any point, a quiet first second, a quiet last second, music with lyrics, a laugh track, a clean studio voice
