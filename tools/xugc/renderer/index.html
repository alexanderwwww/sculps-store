MCP

Connect Claude to XUGC so it can make videos, write Style Bible files and review results for you. It can not change the app's code, your keys or your spending limits, and every video it asks for still goes through your limits.

With “Ask me first” every video Claude orders shows the full prompt and the price on your screen and waits for you.
In claude.ai: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector → paste the URL above → name it XUGC. Then tell Claude: “check XUGC”.
Make a video (any length and quality, inside your limits) · stop it · write, switch or delete Style Bible files · upload a finished video so it can watch it · read the status and spending.

Settings

Spending limits are enforced by the app itself. A job over a limit is refused before anything is rented, and a running job is stopped when it reaches its limit.

Free. The LTX model is gated: make a Hugging Face account, open huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5 and accept the terms, then make a Read token.
Every GPU also deletes itself at its deadline, even if this app is closed.
Without it every video downloads the model again (about 20 minutes of GPU time). With a volume it is already there.

Build 3. Your keys and data live in your Mac's Application Support folder for XUGC, never in the app itself.
