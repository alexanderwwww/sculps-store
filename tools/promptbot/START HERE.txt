BLACK REAPER — IMAGE WAND


FIRST TIME

1. Double-click  wand.command

   Mac will refuse it: "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified
   developer". That's expected. Now go to:

       Apple menu -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security

   Scroll down. There's a line saying wand.command was blocked.
   Click OPEN ANYWAY, enter your password.

   Double-click wand.command again, click Open. That's the last time.

2. If it says Node isn't installed, it opens nodejs.org for you.
   Click the big green LTS button, double-click the .pkg that downloads,
   click through the installer. No warnings on that one - Apple signs it.
   Then double-click wand.command again.

3. It installs itself. A couple of minutes, once only.

4. It asks which one to use:

       1  Gemini
       2  ChatGPT
       3  Both - every prompt through each, saved to separate folders

   Type the number and press Return.

5. A Chrome window opens. LOG IN there.
   It's its own profile - your normal Chrome and your tabs are untouched.
   If you picked 3, log into BOTH, one tab each.

6. Back to the black Terminal window, press Return to start.


EVERY TIME AFTER THAT

   Just double-click wand.command.


WHAT YOU'LL SEE

   A magic wand flies to the message box, types the prompt, sends it, and
   waits for the pictures. A panel bottom-right says where it's up to. It
   moves to the next prompt on its own.

   PRESS ESC IN THE BROWSER TO STOP. Nothing more gets typed.

   Your images land in the  images  folder next to this file.
   Send them to Claude and they go on the store.


THE PROMPTS

   It fetches Claude's latest prompts every time it runs. So when you ask
   Claude for new shots, they're loaded the next time you double-click.

   Want to write your own instead? Edit prompts.txt in TextEdit - one prompt
   per block, a BLANK LINE between blocks - then make an empty file next to
   it called  .prompts-edited  and it'll stop fetching and leave yours alone.


IF IT SAYS CHROME DIDN'T OPEN

   A normal Chrome was still running. Quit it properly - Cmd+Q, not the red
   dot - then double-click wand.command again.
