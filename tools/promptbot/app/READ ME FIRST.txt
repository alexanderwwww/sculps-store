MAGIC WAND


FIRST — throw away the old one

If there's a "wand" folder in Downloads, or an older Magic Wand in
Applications, drag them to the Trash and empty it. Nothing in them is
needed.


INSTALL

1. DRAG  Magic Wand.app  INTO APPLICATIONS. Before opening it.

   Click it once, Cmd+C. Then in the menu bar: Go -> Applications,
   and Cmd+V. Say Replace if it asks.

   Why it matters: macOS runs a downloaded app from a locked copy of
   itself, so it can't finish setting up and you get "Setup failed"
   with no explanation. Moving it first is what stops that.

2. Double-click  Install Node  and let it finish.

   Already have Node? It says so and closes. Otherwise it downloads
   the right version for your Mac and opens Apple's installer:
   Continue, Continue, Agree, Install, your password, Close.

3. Open Magic Wand from Applications. macOS will refuse it:
   "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer."
   That's expected.

4. Apple menu -> System Settings -> Privacy & Security
   Scroll down. There's a line about Magic Wand being blocked.
   Click OPEN ANYWAY, enter your password.

5. Open it again, click Open. Last time it asks.

6. It sets itself up. Two or three minutes, once only.


EVERY TIME

Open Magic Wand. Pick 1 for Gemini or 2 for ChatGPT. A Chrome window
opens — its own, your normal Chrome and your tabs are untouched.
Sign in there if you aren't already. Press Return in the black window
and leave it open.

Then tell Claude what you want, in plain words. Claude writes the
prompts and queues them.


WHEN A JOB ARRIVES

A panel appears in the top-right corner of the browser with the job
name and a box under it:

     Drop your pictures here, or click to choose  ·  up to ten

     [ Skip ]                            [ Submit ]

   Drag your product photos onto that box, or click it to browse.
   Thumbnails appear. Click a thumbnail to take it back out.

   Submit          starts. One prompt at a time: it attaches your
                   pictures, types the full prompt, sends, waits for
                   the pictures to finish generating, saves them,
                   opens a fresh chat, next one.

   Skip            leaves that job alone.

You don't have to add pictures — Submit with an empty box and it runs
on the words alone.

The wand flies to whatever it's about to click and sparkles. While it
waits for a generation it drifts down the right-hand edge, so you can
see it's alive.

PRESS ESC IN THE BROWSER TO STOP. Nothing more gets typed.


WHERE THE PICTURES GO

   Downloads -> Magic Wand -> (a folder per job)

It opens that folder when it finishes.


WATCH FOR THIS ON THE FIRST PROMPT

   attached 1 (from the card)   <- good, your photo went in
   !! nothing attached     <- red. Tell Claude, don't let it run on.


IF IT SAYS CHROME DIDN'T OPEN

A normal Chrome was still running. Quit it properly — Cmd+Q, not the
red dot — then open Magic Wand again.


IF IT SAYS SETUP FAILED

The app isn't in Applications. Drag it there and try again.
