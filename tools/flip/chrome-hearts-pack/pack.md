## How selling on Grailed works (plain language)

Sourced from the pages fetched for this pack; exact current numbers should be confirmed on Grailed's own
**grailed.com/sell** and Help Center, because Grailed blocks automated fetching of those pages (they returned
403 here), so the fee/feature figures below come from current third-party guides that agree with each other —
treat them as "very likely right, confirm the decimals in your own account."

**Listing an item.** Free to list — no listing fee, no store subscription. You upload photos, pick
Designer = *Chrome Hearts*, pick the category (Accessories → Hats / Belts / Jewelry / Sunglasses, or
Footwear → Boots), write a title and description, set Color, Condition and Size, add up to ~10 tags, and set
a price. You can turn on **Smart Pricing**, which automatically drops your price a little each week until it
reaches a floor you set.

**Buy Now vs Offers.** A listing can be bought instantly at your price (**Buy Now**), and buyers can also send
**Offers**. You get 24 hours to accept, counter or decline an offer. You can also **send offers to people who
liked** your item — the usual limit is 2 offers per listing and no more than 1 per 24-hour period, so it's a
nudge, not a spam tool. (This is the honest, allowed version of "send offer to likers" — do it by hand, within
those limits.)

**The fee (~9% + processing).** Listing is free; Grailed takes its cut only when the item sells:
- **Commission ≈ 9%** of the sale price (a reduced ~6% applies on items under $120, which won't matter here —
  everything in this pack is well over $120).
- **Payment processing ≈ 3.49% + $0.49** on a domestic sale once you're onboarded to Grailed Payments
  (Stripe), and ≈ **4.99% + $0.49** international.
- So budget roughly **12–13% all-in** on a US sale. Price with that in mind.

**Grailed Payments / PayPal.** Grailed runs payments through **Grailed Payments (Stripe)** — you onboard once
with your details and payouts land in your bank. **PayPal** is also supported. Either way the money moves
*through Grailed*, which is exactly what keeps you protected. Get payments set up before you list, or a sale
has nowhere to land.

**Shipping and tracking.** You ship it yourself. **Always buy a tracked label and upload the tracking number**
— Grailed (like every marketplace) ties seller protection and payout to a delivery scan, and on high-ticket
pieces use **signature on delivery** and insure for the full value. Ship promptly once paid. Pack the silver
and the sunglasses properly (box, not a soft mailer).

**Promoting.** Grailed's own lever is **relisting / bumping** a listing to push it back to the top of search,
plus the send-offer-to-likers nudge above and Smart Pricing. There is no paid "boost." Good photos, a specific
title, and keeping the piece fresh do most of the work.

**What gets accounts banned or frozen (stay on the safe side of every one):**
- **Counterfeit / replica listings.** Zero tolerance — listing removed, repeat offenders permanently frozen.
  (Not a risk here: everything is genuine. Keep your proof — hallmarks, stamps, the detail photos.)
- **Off-platform deals or sharing contact info** (email/phone/socials) to move the sale off Grailed. Prohibited,
  and it voids all protection. **This is the one to drill into muscle memory.**
- **Automation / bots / scraping.** Grailed bans accounts for automating actions. That is exactly why Alex
  lists and bumps **by hand** — flip/any tool must not touch his Grailed account.
- **Price games / fee avoidance** — fake listings, listing at $1 to move the real deal off-platform, inflating
  then "discounting", shill bidding. All get accounts actioned.
- **Misrepresenting condition or the item.** An over-graded piece becomes a "not as described" claim, which is
  both a refund and a strike. Grade honestly — the condition notes below are written to be safe.
