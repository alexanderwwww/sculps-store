# The arithmetic that decides it

Every recommendation carries this math or it is not a recommendation. The
target is whatever Alex asked for that day — it is never assumed.

## The numbers

- **Price** — what the sellers already charge. Read it off their pages; never
  invent one.
- **Cost** — landed cost, including shipping to the customer. If it is not
  known, say it is not known.
- **Gross margin** = price − cost.
- **Orders needed per day** = target per day ÷ price.
- **Ad budget available per order** = gross margin × 0.5. Half of gross to
  acquisition is the ceiling a small store survives on.
- **The question** is simply: can a customer be bought for that, on this
  platform, for this product? A €9 margin product that needs a €7 CPA is not a
  business. A €40 margin product that needs €20 usually is.

## Worked shape

Target €1,000/day at a €49 price = ~20 orders/day. At a €30 landed margin that
is €600 of gross a day and roughly €15 of ad room per order.

Target €10,000/day at the same price = ~200 orders/day. That is a different
business: it needs stock depth, a supplier who can hold that rate, and a
creative pipeline, and the report must say so rather than pretend the €1k
answer scales by itself.

## The honest failure

If the price the market charges cannot carry the target, write that sentence
plainly. "This product cannot reach €10k/day at €19 without a 15% CPA" is
worth more than a ranked list that quietly ignores the arithmetic.
