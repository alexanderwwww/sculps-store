# What the report has to contain

A report that cannot be acted on the same afternoon is a waste of the sweep.

**Every claim carries its evidence.** A number in the report is a number that
came off a page — with the link next to it. No claim without a source; no
source without a date.

**Say what was not found.** A sweep that saw four advertisers and no clips is
a thin sweep, and the report says thin. The sentence "not enough seen yet" is
always available and always better than confidence that was not earned.

**One recommendation, not a menu.** Lead with the single best candidate and
the reason. Then the runners-up, then everything else seen. Alex decides in
the first ten seconds; the rest is for when he wants to argue with it.

**The arithmetic is in the report**, not implied: price, margin if known,
orders a day to hit his target, ad room per order.

**Say the risks.** Patent, licence, fragility, shipping weight, the size of
the window left. A recommendation that hides its risk gets made once.
