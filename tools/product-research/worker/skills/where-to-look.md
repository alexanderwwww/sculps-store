# Where a sweep goes, and in what order

1. **The Ad Library first.** Spend is the strongest signal, and it decides
   whether the rest of the sweep is worth running.
2. **TikTok search, then the hashtags around it.** Organic reach shows whether
   people want to watch the thing, which is whether the creative can be made
   cheaply.
3. **Instagram hashtags.** Slower-moving, older audience, better for gifting
   and home products.
4. **The sellers' own pages**, last: the price, the offer, the bundle, the
   shipping promise. That is where the arithmetic comes from.

Go at a person's pace. A sweep that hammers the sites gets the app shown
captchas, and a sweep that gets captchas returns nothing at all. Slow and
complete beats fast and blocked.
