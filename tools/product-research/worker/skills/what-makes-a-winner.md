# What makes a product worth selling

A product that sells has a reason, and the reason is visible before you buy
any stock. Judge every candidate against all six. A candidate that fails two
of these does not go in the report as a recommendation, whatever its numbers.

1. **It solves something or it is a gift.** Impulse buys are either a small
   pain removed or a thing somebody hands to another person.
2. **It shows well in three seconds.** If the product does not read on video
   without explanation, paid traffic will not carry it.
3. **The margin survives ads.** See `margin-math.md`. A product that cannot
   pay for its own customer is a hobby.
4. **It is not fragile, not huge, not heavy.** Breakage and shipping eat
   everything. A thing that must arrive perfect and weighs 4kg is a trap.
5. **It is not somebody's patent or somebody's brand.** Licensed characters
   and patented mechanisms are how a store gets shut down mid-season.
6. **There is stock to be had.** A winner you cannot restock in time for the
   season is worse than no winner at all — it burns the ad account's learning
   and the customer's patience.

## Seasonal windows

Seasonal products are judged on a calendar, never in the abstract. The
question is not "does this sell" but "does this sell **in the weeks left**".
Work backwards from the date the customer needs it in hand, subtract the
shipping time, subtract the days it takes ads to learn. What remains is the
window. If the window is under two weeks, say so at the top of the report and
rank on speed, not on ceiling.
