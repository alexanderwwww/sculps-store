## C. THE FIVE ANSWERS

### 1. Most common section order (observed on 8 professionally-built pages)
Gallery → title/price/variants/ATC → trust badges → benefit icons → narrative/feature blocks → **how-it-works steps** → comparison chart → specs → FAQ → reviews-or-related.
- **Buy box is above the fold on 7 of 8**: sodastream, larq, fellow, ember, newair, blendjet, fizzics. The one exception is shop.coopercooler.com, where the price card sits *below* a 14-image gallery — and that is also the least professional page in the set (visible typo).
- **Specs sit late** — last-before-footer on sodastream and newair; mid-page on fellow; after "Inside Ember" on ember. Never above the fold.
- **Comparison chart sits late but BEFORE specs/FAQ**: larq (after tech deep-dive), sodastream (after reviews), fellow (after the visual breakdown), ember (battery chart mid-page).
- **Reviews are not consistent.** Mid-page on sodastream; last-before-footer on shop.coopercooler; near the bottom on newair; **absent from the ember and fizzics PDPs**; on blendjet only a star/count figure (4.9 / 69,439) rather than a review module.

### 2. Explaining a product nobody has seen
Diagram + numbered steps carries it; video supports it, it does not replace it.
- 3-step "How to Use" before anything technical: https://www.livelarq.com/shop/larq-bottle-purevis and https://store.fizzics.com/products/fizzics-draftpour (insert / pull / push).
- 4-step visual guide with icons: https://sodastream.com/products/terra.
- Dedicated technology deep-dive **with a diagram, placed late** (after social proof, before the comparison chart): LARQ PureVis; "Inside Ember": https://ember.com/products/ember-mug-3.
- Video-first is used only on the brand site with no price: demo video is section #1 on https://coopercooler.com/. Video appears mid/low as support on https://shop.coopercooler.com/ and as a brewmaster testimonial on fizzics.
- Pure-image explanation, no copy: 25+ images on the GE Opal PDP.

### 3. Price framing in the $79–149 band
- **$99, single price + Klarna 4 × $24.75 + free shipping over $80**: https://www.livelarq.com/shop/larq-bottle-purevis
- **$149.95, single price, free shipping stated at the buy box, no compare-at**: https://ember.com/products/ember-mug-3
- **$94.99, single price, sale price = regular price, no bundle, no plan**: https://shop.coopercooler.com/
- **$129.99 → $119.99 with a per-colour ladder to $139.99 + stock counts**: https://store.fizzics.com/products/fizzics-draftpour
- **Compare-at + auto-applied bundle + BNPL**: $59.95 vs $109.80, "ADD BUNDLE TO CART", Afterpay 4 × $14.99: https://blendjet.com/products/blendjet-2
- **Three tiers, each with its own compare-at**: $59.99/$89.99 → $69.99/$99.99 → $84.99/$114.99: https://sodastream.com/products/terra. **The second tier adds consumables plus a service convenience (prepaid cylinder-return box), not a second machine.** Fellow's bundles are the same idea framed as "Save $149" / "Save $75": https://fellowproducts.com/products/stagg-ekg-electric-pour-over-kettle. Bartesian's second tier is a capsule add-on at the buy box (+$9.99 to +$24.96): https://bartesian.com/products/cocktail-maker
- Free-shipping thresholds observed: $50 (bartesian), $65 (sodastream), $80 (larq); unconditional free shipping (newair, ember, blendjet).

### 4. The "why not just use a fridge / ice" objection — observed on 5 pages
- **Speed multiples against the named alternative**: "90x faster than refrigerator, 40x faster than freezer" (https://coopercooler.com/); "240 times faster than a refrigerator" (https://poolcandy.store/products/chill-o-matic-automatic-beverage-cooler).
- **Named section that reframes the fridge as the problem**: "Chill-on-Demand™ — Save refrigerator space and get your beverages cold when you want them" (https://coopercooler.com/). GE runs the same play softly: "Free up freezer space" / never "having to plan ahead for ice" (https://www.geappliances.com/ge-appliances/small-appliances/countertop-ice-makers/).
- **Comparison chart listing the cheap alternatives by name** — the strongest version: LARQ vs regular bottles vs filtered bottles vs mercury UV pens (https://www.livelarq.com/shop/larq-bottle-purevis).
- **Pre-empting the failure mode of the DIY route**: "eliminates freezer explosion risks", "will not cause any fizz or foam, ever" (chill-o-matic); "No Foam" as its own section (coopercooler.com).
- **Not handled at all** on https://www.newair.com/products/newair-countertop-ice-maker-nugget-ice-26-lbs-matte-black.

### 5. Above the fold on the pages that feel most professional (7 pages)
Consistently present: large image gallery/carousel; product title; **price**; variant selector; add-to-cart; at least one trust line.
- Trust line at the fold: "Free shipping" + "1-year mug warranty" (ember); "2-Year Warranty Included" + "Free Shipping within the Contiguous U.S." (newair); free shipping over $65 + warranty + "bestseller" badges (sodastream); free shipping over $50 + subscription savings (bartesian); "Free Express Shipping" + ship-by date + Afterpay (blendjet).
- Authority badge at the fold: **Shark Tank logo** (fizzics).
- Tier/bundle selector at the fold, before any copy: sodastream, blendjet.
- Fellow and Ember both put 4 feature icons at or immediately under the fold.

## D. MY INFERENCE (not observed — weigh accordingly)
- cryo is closest in job and price to Cooper Cooler ($94.99) but Cooper Cooler's own store is the weakest page in this set; the template to copy is **LARQ at $99**, because LARQ also sells an invisible mechanism into a market that already owns the free alternative.
- Since cryo's mechanism is not visible from the outside, the LARQ pattern — 3 simple steps early, the real diagram late, then a comparison chart naming fridge / freezer / ice / cooler — is likely to outperform a video-first page for cold traffic.
- A second tier for cryo has no consumable to sell (no cartridge, by the brief), so the SodaStream/Bartesian tier model does not port directly; the nearest analogue is a service/convenience add (extra bottle, longer warranty) rather than a 2-pack.
- $89 with a stated free-shipping line and a BNPL split (~4 × $22.25) matches the band's convention; a compare-at only works if a real higher price exists first.
- None of this is proof about conversion — these are layout observations, not test results. No page in this set published A/B data.
