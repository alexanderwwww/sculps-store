# cryo — the eight carousel panel prompts

**Reference image to attach to the job (once, at the start):**
`https://kerberos.gardenbuddystore.workers.dev/media/cryo-reference-18cc10be0cbc.webp`

Run as one Magic Wand job, eight prompts, reference attached once, `newChat: "start"` at
the beginning only. Then render → look at it → place it. Never place a panel unseen.

**Colour system for this product.** The Halloween palette in `look.md` is that season's;
cryo's is two colours plus black: **ice blue** (the lead accent, the one doing the work)
and **white**, on a **deep charcoal / near-black** ground, with **brushed silver** coming
from the product itself. No orange, no green, no violet, no gradient wash.

**Two rules that apply to all eight and are written into every prompt below:**
1. **The bottle is unbranded and blank.** No label, no logo, no printed text, no brand,
   no shrink wrap, no colour band. Clear plastic and water. It is in the banned list of
   every single panel.
2. **No time claim anywhere.** No seconds, no minutes, no "instantly", no "fast". The
   chill time is not measured. No number of any kind appears on a panel unless it is in
   that panel's permitted word list, and every permitted number below is a measured or
   physical fact (size, temperature range, the dial's range).

## Before placing any panel

Shrink it to 64 pixels and look: is the machine still a recognisable shape, is the
headline still readable, would it stop a thumb. Then check the two product-specific
things that will fail first — **is the bottle blank**, and **is there a time, a number of
seconds or a stopwatch anywhere in the frame**. Either one, patch the prompt, POST the
same job `id`, `requeue`, `redo N`.
