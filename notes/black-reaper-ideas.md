## The mum-and-daughter iMessage

The Ceiling Buddy blue-screen treatment — a phone thread rendered as an image
on the page, and the same script shot as a TikTok. Works because the joke
isn't about the product; the product is what the joke is about.

**Daughter:** hey baby how are you

**Mum:** sorry for the late reply, im a little overstimulated rn

**Mum:** your dad bought a 13 foot ghost. its fighting in the yard

**Daughter:** WHAT

**Mum:** *[photo of dad, delighted, standing next to it]*

The last beat is the whole thing: no punchline in text, just the picture of a
grown man who has never been happier. The daughter never replies again.

Two places it goes:
- **On the page**, as a still, in the existing phone frame
- **As a TikTok**, the same thread typing itself out, then the photo lands

Why it sells: nobody in it is selling. It's a family being embarrassed by a
dad, which is the exact feeling the product actually produces. The comments
write themselves — everybody has that dad or is that dad.

Shot list for the photo: dad mid-gesture at the base of the inflatable,
genuinely thrilled, slightly too pleased with himself. Ordinary clothes,
ordinary yard, phone-camera quality. It must look like the mum took it while
sighing.

Variants worth trying: the 13-foot ghost, the Scream, the reaper archway.
Whichever is funniest is whichever is biggest.

## Standing rule: no placeholder data. Ever.

Not on the live site, not on a preview URL, not "we'll swap it later", not
behind a flag. No invented reviews, no filler names, no lorem, no example
products. If a section has nothing real to show it stays empty until it does.
