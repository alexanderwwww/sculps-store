# $15 → $2,000, broken into micro-goals (Alex, 2026-10-20)

Each micro-goal is ×φ (1.618) on the last one. There are 11 steps:

$15 → $24.3 → $39.3 → $63.6 → $102.9 → $166.5 → $269.4 → $435.9 → $705.3 → $1,141 → $1,846 → $2,000

## The equation for every trade
- Stake = 23.6% of the current balance (φ⁻³). On $15 that is $3.54. The stake grows only as the balance grows. Never more after a loss.
- Win = +20% on the stake, loss = −15% stop. A round trip costs about 2% in fees.
- One win grows the balance ×1.0425, and one loss shrinks it ×0.960.
- **Break-even win rate: 49.6%.** Below that, the balance shrinks however many trades are taken.

| Win rate | Trades to reach $2,000 |
|---|---|
| 50% | ~14,900 (barely moving) |
| 55% | ~1,100 |
| 60% | ~570 |
| 65% | ~384 |

## What this means
Symbiot is at 19% wins on everything it buys. Its job is to find the kind of coin and setup that wins **55% or more** after fees. Only that setup gets real money, one micro-goal at a time. If a step goes backwards, the stake drops back to the step before.
