# THE SCREAM — Meta carousel study (marketing team)
Source of every fact: the live product page blackreaper.us/products/the-scream, read 2026-10-02. Nothing below is invented.

## 1. The product, as the page sells it
- 16 ft 4 in / 5 m ghost. Screaming white mask, blade in hand, lit from inside. "The whole street sees him." Head level with the upstairs windows.
- Price **$299.99**. Code **REAPER20** = $20 off, so **$279.99** at checkout. 10 ft version $229.99. "Only 25 left." 4.9 rating, 719+ buyers.
- Free US shipping. Ships in 1-2 business days, arrives 5-7 days after. 30-day returns. Tracking link on every order.
- **Deadline:** order by **October 20** to be at the door before Halloween.
- Box: blower unit with sealed outdoor lead, six 10-inch steel ground stakes, four guy ropes with tensioners, repair patch kit. Weatherproof nylon. LEDs inside head and body.
- Up in about 90 seconds (silent fan runs the whole time). Two people, about 15 minutes with staking. Power draw about a table lamp. Folds into the bag it arrived in, one person can carry it, garage until next October.
- Wind: staked properly it handles an ordinary autumn night; in a storm, unplug it and it lays down flat. (Never promise "storm-proof".)

## 2. Who buys and why
US homeowner, 28-55, decorates big every year. Not buying a decoration: buying **being the house**. The reward is social: neighbours stopping, people filming, kids pointing, the delivery driver turning back. Secondary buyer: the dad who wants the one thing the street will remember. Halloween spend is emotional and deadline-driven: it cannot be bought on November 1.

## 5. Meta rules for this ad
- ONE ad set, ABO, ONE carousel ad. 8 cards, square 1:1, 1080x1080.
- Card 1 decides everything: it must work with zero context. Keep text per card to a handful of big words; the figure stays the hero and uncovered.
- Each card must look like a different deliberately designed ad, not the same template eight times.
- Generic only: no real news or company logos, no invented event. A "BREAKING" style is allowed only as a generic format with an "Ad" tag.
- Real-person quotes: only the lines already on the product page; no new fake testimonials, no invented numbers, no "storm-proof", no "world's tallest".
- True size: head at the upstairs windows, a grown man about one third of its height. Never exaggerate.

## 6. Card-by-card creative job (for the image maker)
1. Stop the scroll: the strongest single image of the figure; make people swipe.
2. Status: the street has stopped for this house.
3. Scale: 16 ft 4 in made unmistakable against people and the house.
4. Ease: the box on the doorstep to standing, 90 seconds.
5. The moment: inflating, head lighting red.
6. What you get: the product as hero plus the box contents.
7. Street reaction: neighbours, phones, kids far away, the driver turning back.
8. Close: why now (Oct 20), $279.99, free shipping, 30-day returns, a clear button.
