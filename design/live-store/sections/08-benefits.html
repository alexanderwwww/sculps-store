Why people keep one

Kneel. Flip. Sit.

One frame does the whole job. Pad side up to kneel, flipped over to sit.

  • Kneel one way, sit the other

    Pad side up to kneel. Flip it over and the frame is a seat at bed height.

  • Handles to stand up

    Getting down is easy. The steel handles give you something solid to push on.

  • Arrives assembled, folds flat

    Open the box and unfold it. Folded, it hangs on a nail in the shed.
