Where we ship

We currently ship to the United States only.

Rates

  • 3-month and 6-month supplies: free shipping, always.
  • 1-month supply: flat-rate standard shipping, shown at checkout.

Timing

Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day; otherwise the next business day. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email the moment your box ships.

Issues

Lost or stuck in transit for more than 10 business days? Contact us with your order number and we reship or refund — your choice.
