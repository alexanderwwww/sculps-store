30-day money-back guarantee

Try lockin for a full 30 days. If it's not for you, email us within 30 days of delivery — you get a full refund of the product price. No forms, no return-shipping games, no arguing.

How it works

  • Contact us via the contact page or reply to your order confirmation email, and include your order number.

  • You don't need to ship anything back for single-box orders. For multi-box supplies (3 or 6 months), we may ask you to return the unopened boxes — we send a prepaid label.

Damaged or wrong items
