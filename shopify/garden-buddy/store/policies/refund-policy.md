# Refund and Return Policy

## 30 days

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to request a return for any reason. If you do not get on with it, send it back and we refund you in full.

## Condition

The item must be in the condition it arrived in, aside from normal garden use — a bit of soil on the pad is fine, a bent frame is not. You do not need the original box.

## How to start a return

Email gardenbuddystore@gmail.com with your order number and a sentence about why. We reply within one business day with a prepaid return label. Print it, attach it to any box, and drop it at the carrier.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free. We provide the label.

## Refunds

Once the returned item reaches us and is inspected, we issue the refund to your original payment method. Refunds are processed within 2 business days of receipt, and your bank typically posts them within 3–5 business days after that.

You will receive an email when the refund is issued.

## Damaged or faulty on arrival

Send a photo to gardenbuddystore@gmail.com. We send a replacement at no cost and you keep or dispose of the damaged one. No return needed.

## Wrong item

If we sent the wrong bundle, tell us and we ship the correct one immediately with a prepaid label for the wrong one.

## Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before dispatch, usually within 1–2 business days of ordering. Email us as soon as possible. Once it has dispatched, treat it as a return.

## Exchanges

We do not run exchanges as a separate process. Return the item for a refund and place a new order — it is faster.

## Non-returnable

Nothing. Every item we sell is covered by this policy.

## Questions

Email gardenbuddystore@gmail.com.
