# Shipping Policy

Garden Buddy ships to addresses within the United States only. We do not ship internationally at this time.

## Cost

Shipping is free on every order. There are no handling fees and no surcharges at checkout.

## Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of being placed. Orders placed on a weekend or a US public holiday begin processing the next business day.

## Delivery time

Once dispatched, delivery typically takes 2–5 business days. Total time from order to doorstep is usually 3–7 business days.

Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Carrier delays, weather and holiday volume can extend them.

## Tracking

You receive a confirmation email when you order, and a second email with a tracking link as soon as the parcel is dispatched. If you have not received a tracking link within 3 business days, email us and we will find your order.

## Wrong or incomplete address

We ship to the address entered at checkout. If it is wrong, email us immediately — if the parcel has not dispatched, we can correct it. Once dispatched, an incorrect address may mean the parcel is returned to us, and we will reship it at no cost to you once it arrives back.

## Lost or damaged in transit

If your parcel is marked delivered but you do not have it, wait 48 hours (carriers sometimes scan early) then contact us and we will open a claim.

If it arrives damaged, send us a photo. We replace it at no cost. You do not need to return the damaged one.

## PO boxes and APO/FPO

We can ship to PO boxes. APO and FPO addresses may take considerably longer.
