## THE WINNING CONCEPT (the "node")
UGC talking ad. A man, deadpan, gliding across a pool on the Super Glide, flexing.
Angle = **WOW / main-character flex**, NOT a feature pitch.

### Master script — deconstructed into 6 swappable modules
| # | Module | The words |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | **HOOK** | "I will not be taking questions." |
| 2 | **RELATE / FLEX** | "I pull up with a remote-control couch on the water — you think anything can hurt me right now???" |

Once this wins, you never rewrite the whole thing. You swap ONE module = new ad.

1. **Launch the master concept** (#1 hook) + the 2 cuts already made (clip 1 stress, clip 2 flex).
2. **Add 3–4 free variations now** — B-roll swaps (Batch 1) + hook swaps (Batch 2). These are just edits of footage you ALREADY have — $0, no Higgsfield credits needed.
