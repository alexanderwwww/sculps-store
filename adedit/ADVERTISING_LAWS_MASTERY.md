## PART 1 — THE TIMELESS LAWS (the legends)

### Claude Hopkins — *Scientific Advertising* (1923)
The father of measurable advertising. Laws:
- **Advertising is salesmanship in print. Judge it by sales, not applause.** → Our metric is ROAS/CPA, not likes.
- **Specifics beat generalities.** "Holds 300 lb / lasts all afternoon / inflates in 5 min" beats "high quality." → Our PROOF module is all numbers.
- **Tell, don't boast.** Demonstrate the motor moving him; don't say "amazing."
- **Sampling / risk reversal sells.** → Our 30-day money-back guarantee.

### John E. Kennedy (1904) — "Reason why" advertising
Every claim needs a *reason why* it's true. → "It glides because it has twin thrusters" — give the mechanism, the brain believes it.

### Rosser Reeves — *Reality in Advertising* (1961) — the **USP**
One ad = one **Unique Selling Proposition** the competitor can't/doesn't say.
→ Ours: **"A motorized lounger you steer with joysticks — no paddling, ever."** A normal pool float literally cannot say this. Own it.

### David Ogilvy — *Confessions / Ogilvy on Advertising*
- **"When you haven't got a story, make the product the hero."** → The Super Glide IS the spectacle. Open on it moving.
- **5x more people read the headline than the body. The headline is 80% of the spend.** → Our HOOK is the whole game.
- **"The consumer isn't a moron, she's your wife."** Respect them, don't shout.
- **Research the product obsessively** (he read everything before Rolls-Royce). → That's why we analyzed every spec.

### John Caples — *Tested Advertising Methods*
- **The headline is everything; test headlines above all.** → That's literally the 1→50 hook-swap batch.
- **Best headlines appeal to SELF-INTEREST or NEWS or CURIOSITY.** → "I will not be taking questions" = curiosity. "What do you mean you still paddle?" = self-interest/status.
- **"They laughed when I sat down at the piano…"** — story + transformation outsells features.

### Eugene Schwartz — *Breakthrough Advertising* (the deepest book)
- **You cannot create desire — you channel existing desire onto your product.** → People already want status, fun, summer envy. We attach those to the float. We don't invent "wanting to glide."
- **Market Awareness (5 stages):** Unaware → Problem-aware → Solution-aware → Product-aware → Most-aware. Cold TikTok traffic is **Unaware/Problem-unaware** → lead with the *spectacle and identity*, not the product name or price. Warm/retargeting → lead with offer.
- **Market Sophistication (5 stages):** As a market sees the same claim repeated, you must escalate: 1) plain claim → 2) bigger claim → 3) mechanism → 4) new mechanism → 5) identification/identity. Pool floats are an old market → we win at **stage 5: identity** ("be the main character"), backed by **stage 3–4 mechanism** ("twin thrusters").

### Robert Collier — *The Robert Collier Letter Book*
- **"Enter the conversation already happening in the customer's mind."** → In summer, the conversation is "how do I have the best summer / out-flex everyone." We enter THAT.

### Joseph Sugarman — *The Adweapon / Triggers*
- **The sole purpose of the first sentence is to get them to read the second.** Every line buys the next. → Our hook → "you think anything can hurt me" → "most viral product" — a **slippery slide.**
- **Sell the concept, not the product** (sell the lifestyle, the feeling).
- **Emotion sells; logic justifies.** Lead emotional (flex/fun), close with logic (specs, guarantee, price).

### Bill Bernbach (DDB, "Think Small," "Lemon")
- **Creativity & disruption beat repetition. Be the pattern interrupt.** → A grown man silently motoring across a pool IS the disruption.
- **Execution is content.** *How* you say it is the message.

### Gary Halbert — *The Boron Letters*
- **"The most important skill is finding a starving crowd."** Market > copy > offer. → Our starving crowd: men who want summer flex + dads who want a toy. Already starving.
- **AIDA still rules:** Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.

### Drayton Bird / classic DM
- **Offer + urgency + risk-reversal + guarantee** is the conversion stack. → "Half price today" (offer+urgency) + "30-day money-back" (risk-reversal).

### Cialdini — *Influence* (the 7 levers of persuasion)
Reciprocity · **Scarcity** (limited stock / today only) · **Authority** (3,900 reviews / "most viral") · **Social proof** (heads turning, crowds) · Liking (relatable funny guy) · **Commitment/consistency** · **Unity** (this is for *us*, the unbothered). → Bake at least 3 into every ad.

## PART 2 — THE PLATFORM PHYSICS (Meta + TikTok, modern layer)

### The universal short-form law: THE 3-SECOND HOOK
- ~65% of viewers decide to keep watching in the first 3 seconds. **Hook rate (3s view %)** is the #1 lever. Optimize the first frame and first line above all else.
- **Open on motion / mid-action**, never on a logo, title card, or slow build.

### TikTok mechanics
- **Native > polished.** Raw iPhone, vertical 9:16, real settings outperform studio ads. The "ad" must not look like an ad.
- **Watch time + completion + rewatch** drive the For You Page. Keep it ≤20–25s, loop-able. Make the last frame want a rewatch.
- **Captions on-screen** (sound-off viewing) + trending audio (sound-on boost). Both.
- **Comments are ranking fuel.** A mildly debatable hook ("you still paddle?") farms comments = reach.
- **Spark Ads:** boost the organic post that already got traction — keeps social proof (likes/comments) on the ad.
- **Hook formats that win:** POV, "tell me why," "what do you mean you…," text-on-screen reveal, green-screen react.

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) mechanics — current era
- **Advantage+ / AI-led, BROAD targeting wins.** The creative IS the targeting now. Don't hand-pick interests on cold prospecting — let the algo find buyers; the creative self-selects the audience.
- **Optimize for Purchase** (not ATC) once the pixel has data; ATC only if too few purchase events.
- **Creative volume = the input.** Meta needs many creatives to find the winner — exactly why the 1→50 engine exists.
- **CBO / Advantage+ Shopping (ASC)** for budget efficiency at scale; ABO for clean early testing.
- **3-second video views, thumbstop ratio, hold rate, CTR (outbound), CPM** are the diagnostic stack:
  - Low **hook rate** → fix first 3s.
  - Good hook, low **hold** → fix the middle (pacing/proof).
  - Good hold, low **CTR** → fix the CTA/offer.
  - High CTR, low **CVR** → fix the landing page, not the ad.
- **Creative fatigue** shows as rising CPM / falling CTR over 1–2 weeks → that's the trigger to deploy the next batch from the engine.
- **Don't kill ads in the learning phase** (≈50 conversions/week per ad set). Give it data before judging.

### The diagnostic chain (where to fix what)
Scroll-stop (first frame) → Hook (first line) → Hold (middle) → Click (CTA/offer) → Convert (landing page). Each metric points at exactly one module to swap. This is why modular ads (Part 1 of the engine) are testable.

- ✅ **Hook/curiosity (Caples, 3s law):** opens mid-glide, curiosity gap.
- ✅ **Product is the hero (Ogilvy):** the motor moving him is the spectacle.
- ✅ **USP (Reeves):** joystick-steered, no paddling — uncontestable.
- ✅ **Channels existing desire + identity stage (Schwartz):** attaches summer-flex/main-character desire; wins the old float market at the identity level.
- ✅ **Slippery slide (Sugarman):** each line forces the next.
- ✅ **Specifics (Hopkins):** 300 lb, 5 min, all afternoon, twin motors.
- ✅ **Emotion then logic (Sugarman):** flex first, specs + guarantee after.
- ✅ **Cialdini:** social proof (crowd), scarcity ("today"), authority ("most viral"), unity ("us, the unbothered").
- ✅ **Native + captions + loopable (TikTok):** raw pool footage, on-screen text, ≤24s.
- ✅ **Broad-ready (Meta):** the creative self-selects the buyer; no interest stacking needed.

### The law to tattoo on the brand
> Enter the conversation in their head (summer flex) → stop the thumb in 3s with the moving float → channel desire onto the product via identity ("main character") → prove it with specifics → reverse risk + add urgency → make it native and loopable → then manufacture 50 variants and let the algorithm pick the winner.
