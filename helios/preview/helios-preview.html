Before you buy

Questions

How fast does it go?

It is a cruising speed, not a jet ski — enough to move you across a pool or along a shoreline without paddling. The joystick gives you forward, reverse and spin.

How long does the battery last?

3–4 hours of use per charge. Runtime depends on rider weight, water conditions and how continuously you run the motors. The charger is in the box.

Do I need a pump?

No. The inflation nozzle is included and setup takes about five minutes.

What weight can it hold?

Up to 300 lb. Above that the lounger still floats but the motors lose noticeable push.

Can I use it in the sea?

Yes, in calm and sheltered water. Rinse it with fresh water afterwards so salt does not sit on the motors. It is not built for surf or open water.

When will it arrive?

7–10 business days in the US, with tracking emailed to you when it ships.

What if I do not like it?

Send it back within 30 days, unused and in the box, and we refund it. No forms and no return games.
