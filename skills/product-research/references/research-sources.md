# Research Sources & How to Pull Them

<!-- The free/legit data layer this skill orchestrates. Claude is the analysis brain. -->

## Free & official (start here)
| Source | URL | Use for |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Meta Ad Library | facebook.com/ads/library | Every live ad a competitor runs; spot 30+ day winners |
| TikTok Creative Center | ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter | Top ads, trending products/hashtags/sounds by country |
| Google Trends | trends.google.com | Demand direction + seasonality |
| Reddit / forums | reddit.com search | Raw pain/desire language, objections |
| Competitor reviews | their PDP (Judge.me/Loox), Amazon | Voice-of-customer mining |

## Paid ad-spy (optional "data layer")
Foreplay · Minea · PiPiADS · Atria · BigSpy. Workflow: export 10 winning ads (running 30+ days)
→ paste URLs/screenshots here → skill extracts hook/angle/format/offer → briefs.

## Live store data (if Shopify MCP connected)
Pull real catalog, AOV, best-sellers, conversion funnel, and orders to ground every
recommendation in *your* numbers instead of assumptions. Always prefer this over guesses.

## Honest limits
- Meta Ad Library and TikTok Creative Center resist automated scraping; expect to fetch
  a handful manually or have the user paste exports. Don't claim full-catalog scraping.
- No free tool reliably reveals competitor *revenue* — infer from ad longevity + review
  velocity, and label it as an estimate.

## Triangulation rule
A finding is "Strong" only if confirmed by ≥2 independent sources. Otherwise label it
Moderate or Weak so the user knows how hard to lean on it.
